# Stress/dynamics solvability handoff

All **six practical ranking stresses have trained GAN witnesses**, using different configurations. No shared card passed all six; the previously seen alternating-critic cadence remains unsolved. Read [findings](FINDINGS.md), the [configuration × task matrix](MATRIX.md), and the [complete attempt table](attempts.md).

This bundle contains **66 actual GAN episodes**, **1,584 live observations**, and **888.823 seconds** summed recorded episode wall time on a shared CPU host. Each episode uses seed 0, the original numerical bounds, 24 fixed observations and a final passing suffix of at least five. EMA is separate. Thresholds, task importance tiers, and original failures remain unchanged. Seen cadence is development evidence, not a fresh holdout.

The last slow-critic witness keeps the original discriminator LR multiplier .75, updates G every second outer step, and runs 6,000 outer steps: D 6,000 / G 3,000 actual updates. It passes the last 11/24 observations. Other witnesses and their compute differences appear in the matrix. The seven finite target-sample positive controls are scoring checks, not GAN training episodes.

[refine_screen.py](scripts/refine_screen.py) and its four [refinement cards](refinement_cards.json.gz) were **never executed**. They are retained as abandoned plans and do not contribute to the episode count.

The same-budget fast-critic correction is prior LR 30. Four stresses pass with three times the original update budget, and the R1+R2 witness uses a larger architecture and particle table plus longer training. These are per-task solvability findings, not evidence of a universally superior default. The task-specific R1+R2 arm and coefficient remain unchanged when applying cap-only tuning cards.
