## What the metrics show

**Late annealing:** ring acquisition never satisfies the full covariance gate across 96 checks. Endpoint full-component covariance error is 2.926377 against <=.85, versus control .496567. All 16 modes remain; endpoint HQ .897705, mass TV .084473 and minimum eigen ratio .554981 pass. Core covariance .839831 masks contamination in the full distribution. The saved task horizon is **400 within 1,600 acquisition updates**: decay starts at 240, reaches network .2/prior .1 floors by 400, then stays there. The trajectories agree through update 234 and diverge at the next evaluation. This rejects this retained-horizon schedule; simultaneous G/D/prior decay does not isolate a responsible role or rule out other annealing designs.

**Half G/E motion:** ring full-component covariance improves .496567 -> .296711 and the passing suffix grows 26 -> 57. However, two-pole endpoint critic slope 1.007497 exceeds <=1, despite sufficient generator displacement. Its maximum passing run is four, versus control's 17. Unused-token inactive-coordinate retention improves .986702 -> .993585, and endpoint concept movement .948010 passes. Intermediate movement dips below .85, leaving only three terminal passing checks against five required. Endpoint improvements do not establish sustained qualification; D/prior rates remain .018/.030 while G/E travel halves.

**Prior smoothing .01:** ring covariance error .984534 exceeds .85 at every check (best .933245). All 16 modes, endpoint HQ .889893, mass TV .072266 and minimum eigen ratio .169440 pass, but eigen shape also fails at some late checks. Ring sampled-row RMS displacement falls .028211 -> .015171 (46.22%); saved projection/blend counters are zero, so this is final applied motion. Damping occurred on the changed trajectory without curing density. Gaussian acquisition confirms earlier, at 125 versus 375, but endpoint KS worsens .071842 -> .171800 and width ratio falls 1.063043 -> .748965. The preregistered 1,000->6,000 continuation motion forecast is **unavailable** because its Tier 2 continuation is blocked; prefix reductions do not evaluate it.

**Weak critic damping:** all six required acquisition/retention gates pass. Ring mass TV improves .065186 -> .045898, but covariance worsens .496567 -> .806320, still under .85. In Tier 2 it adds **zero** passes and retains eight of the control's nine original-profile passes. Gaussian stability stationary passing checks improve 2/72 -> 15/72 and shifted hold 0/24 -> 3/24, but all-check retention and deadline reacquisition still fail. Final KS improves .320623 -> .181474, still above .05, while mean error worsens .255238 -> .477081, above .2. Mode hold passes 0/24 checks and never reaches eight modes (maximum seven), whereas control has a late quality failure with only three terminal passes against five required.

Word hold passes its final endpoint but fails two confirmed intermediate checks: generation reaches only four modes at updates 2334 and 4167; inverse minimum probability also falls to .792185 below .9 at 4167. Control passes 25/25 checks; hybrid passes 23/25. Each restores its verified own producer exactly, adds 4,000 updates and retains horizon 20,000 without history reset. **Prefixes differ: control 834->4834, hybrid 1667->5667.** The existing acquired-prefix gate is retained as a separately scoped original-gate cohort, not a same-clock causal hold comparison. Equal complete-stream consumption is unverified. On the other **20 Tier 2 tasks with matched complete budgets and consumed training streams**, both recipes have the same eight passes: no added repair, independently of the unmatched word-hold outcome.

Cap/damping term magnitudes and relative forces are **unavailable** in actual host diagnostics: the penalty execution counts and .05 recipe are recorded, but these hosts did not request separate term collection. Whole-prefix motion reductions are changed-trajectory observations, not common-state force probes.

## Recommendation

**Stop all four fixed revisions; none improves the matched Tier 2 pass set.** Preserve the current control. Keep local ring improvements as evidence, but do not promote a recipe that loses required acquisition or retention. Annealing research must explicitly inspect original schedule horizons and continuation semantics before choosing a decay window. The smoothing counters support reduced prior travel but not improved sustained density; smaller motion by itself is insufficient. Do not pay for unchanged reruns, seeds, or deeper ordinary work on rejected revisions.
