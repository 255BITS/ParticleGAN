## What was built (pkg-E14 -> pkg-E15 -> pkg-E16 -> pkg-E17; every step a copy plus one change)
`pkg-E14` = the previous candidate E14s (native gates 3/3 at 7k/14k/28k; row-evidence gate, anchored release, critic-space birth-death, served averaged model). `pkg-E17/particlegan/birth_death.py` adds, behind recipe flag `birth_death_isolation` (default False = bit-identical to pkg-E14, tested), a **support test with re-draw** of table rows, run at every birth-death evaluation (once per turnover of the FIFO reservoir of the last N real rows, about every 10 steps at N = 20,000):
1. `_isolated(q, R, k, fast)`: features of the table rows `q` (clean centres G(z_i) through the critic's learned score heads) and of the real reservoir `R` (the same critic). Split R into halves R1 (reference) / R2 (calibration). rho(j) = leave-one-out k-th neighbour distance of reference point j. Score of a point u = a(u) / median over its k nearest reference points of rho, where a(u) = distance to its k-th nearest reference point (a local, scale-free isolation ratio: sparse-but-real regions are compared with themselves). p_i = (1 + #{R2 scores >= score_i}) / (1 + |R2|) (split-conformal: valid when the fake law equals the real law). Benjamini-Hochberg at Q = .05 over the N rows. k = ceil((log2(N/Q)+1)/2) (10 at N = 20,000), already the birth-death's k.
2. `_isolation_pick`: flagged rows (that ordinary birth-death did not just move) are re-drawn as clones (`_move`: the parent's latent + the package's tiny latent jitter, EMA row, Adam/AMSGrad rows, history) of a uniformly drawn **unflagged** row among those at most twice as far (in the table's own latent space z) as the flagged row's nearest unflagged row. Nothing is done while more than Q of the table is flagged (mostly in transit: no support to clone from). Moved rows are re-anchored/invalidated in the table tester and their row evidence is reset (`training.py` uses `birth_death.moved_rows`).
Versions: E15 = uniform random parents (bad: fails 0/3 on accuracy: mass shuffled at random, tester ladder stalls); E16 = one of the k nearest unflagged rows as parent (3/3 pass; parents are rim rows, 3,833 unique rows re-drawn 17,756 times on rotated); **E17 = the ball rule above (3/3 pass, best margins)**. E17a = E17 + row-evidence gate (overrides-E17.json); E17b = E17 without the gate (overrides-E17b.json; the candidate we would prefer: fewer chosen constants).
