# Five independent R1/R2 repair tracks

The user requested five further subagents after the closed two-round search.
This is a separately registered, single-round campaign: five new whole recipes
and the exact cached scheduled baseline. The previous registration, failures,
stopping decision and archived results retain their identities.

All five tracks read the compiled experiment memory and saved density diagnosis.
Each proposes one existing public-API trainer mechanism: critic timescale, prior
mobility, optimizer denominator memory, generator transport or momentum. Each
proposal declares its exact measured parent, effective owned delta, falsifiable
prediction and competing explanation. The full resolved recipes and READY
studies, rather than the track names, specify the admitted experiment.

Every recipe uses both R1/R2 penalties on every update. The protocol keeps seed
0, public deterministic initialization, task architectures and target/data laws,
actual initial tensors and ordered target batches, initial prior distribution
and capacity, sampling, update limits, evaluation cadence and numerical gates.
Recipe prior-update policies and regularizers remain explicit. Each candidate
faces all six required ordinary Tier 1 tasks plus its separate clock measurement
under one global recipe. Fixed identity/zero fixtures retain separate cohorts.

A repair requires 6/6 ordinary PASS and its own clock PASS. An improved Ring16
endpoint, an acquisition checkpoint or a mixture of task-specific recipes is
insufficient. Ring16 still requires the full component covariance error <=0.85,
all other density bounds and at least five passing terminal observations. Pole
transport retains its sustained gate. No changed tasks, longer updates, seed
variants, paid retries, automatic refinement or Tier 2 work are admitted.

Full reservations total 15,120 seconds for six roles, 2,520 per role. The shared
queue paid ceiling is 5,400 seconds, including the previous 1,804.5319410728698
seconds: new work has at most 3,595.4680589271302 seconds of paid headroom. Each
next job needs its full original reservation; a cap leaves explicit unmeasured
cells rather than shortening a run. All prior charges and attempts remain in
the original queue. The seven exact baseline jobs reuse certified evidence at
zero new cost. The new archive links that queue and has its own immutable
admission receipt; both archives together retain the 8 GiB cap and 5 GiB
free-space floor. One worker runs per A6000 GPU, with one CPU thread.
