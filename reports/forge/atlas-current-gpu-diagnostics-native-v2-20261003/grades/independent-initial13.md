## What the failures actually show

**Two-pole measures bounded travel and critic slope, not full two-pole distribution recovery.** The particle position never reaches the `.3` travel gate: its recorded range is `.00515` to `.03893`. Small critic slopes are insufficient to move the table. The record alone does not isolate the optimizer/controller cause.

**Ring16 recovers coarse occupancy but not narrow component fidelity.** Its best recorded HQ is only `.64624`, and even its best full-component covariance error is `4.23666`, above `.85`. All 16 endpoint component errors exceed the bound. A visible ring and balanced mass therefore do not demonstrate the declared Gaussian-cluster question.

**Unequal-mass and unequal-width localize a spread failure missed by global tests.** Unequal-mass's last component has 98/4096 assignments, covariance error `1.89890`, and is not locally resolved; the average full-component error is `.86217`. Unequal-width's component errors are `[10.58568, 4.09623, .99410, .10188]`. Its clipped-core covariance diagnostic is `.20481`, while its required full-sample covariance is `3.94447`. The distinction implicates tails/outliers in the measured failure, but does not identify their generating mechanism. Replacing the full gate with the core diagnostic would change the question.

**Bars4 and blobs4 fail different image questions.** Bars4 produces sharp tiles while omitting one mode and strongly misallocating others; only two modes meet the original qualifying definition despite three nonzero nearest-mode fractions. Blobs4 produces nearly black tiles throughout the inspected GIF, with no qualifying template matches. Critic-rate adaptation, saturation, row allocation and latent geometry remain hypotheses until a separately declared causal test isolates them.

**The seven passing terminal suffixes have different histories.** Overlap first passes at update 150 but its retained suffix begins at 650; intensity first passes at 350 but its suffix begins at 425. Their original PASS must not be relabeled uninterrupted first-window hold, shipping robustness or fastest convergence. Recorded confirmation times in seconds are unavailable.
