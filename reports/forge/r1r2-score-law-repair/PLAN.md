## One supported contrast, with weak confidence

The [primary-source review](../r1r2-objective-memory-repair/research/score-law-literature.md)
and [saved-score diagnostic](../r1r2-objective-memory-repair/research/score-law/diagnosis.json)
support testing **only `Recipe.loss: relativistic → non_saturating`** on exact
measured AMSGrad m110. Paired critic loss and both R1/R2 penalties are invariant
to a common score shift. Native logistic classification anchors absolute scores,
changing early critic dynamics from the shared initialization. Its generator
and joint inverse-encoder score weights also change, so a result cannot isolate
critic anchoring alone.

This is not an endpoint gradient-starvation repair: saved paired and native
non-saturating forces are nearly collinear, and the absolute-score residual is
small. Positive scalar reweighting cannot rotate that saved critic's spatial
gradient. Locally similar centered-score curvature and R3GAN's different-cohort
paired-coverage advantage are negative evidence. A benefit would have to develop
through the changed training trajectory and satisfy the original density gate.
No least-squares arm, penalty-strength grid or transport composition follows.
The [full-transport review](../r1r2-objective-memory-repair/research/full-transport.md)
does not select a shared entropy/scale rule and admits no extra arm.
