## What the new pass establishes

Transport-v1 already repaired allocation but missed local variance at updates 1050 and 1100. The successor's last-five minimum eigen ratios are **.478403, .159678, .442112, .486961, .366567**, all above the retained **.15** floor. The complete covariance, mass, distribution and quality bounds pass together at those five observations. Final full covariance improves **.773642 → .522577**, while the passing broad-vector guardrail retains all 24 checks.

This is a sustained rare-density improvement, rather than universal mass improvement. Rare centers change **5 → 6** and rare served mass **96/4096 → 114/4096**, against target .02; overall mass TV slightly worsens **.013496 → .016426**. The narrow margin **.159678** does not establish wider robustness. Both arms see the same intermittent rare observations, so this successful comparison shows that those batches can support the gate under the changed trainer; it does not identify every cause of the predecessor's failure.

Unequal width remains unresolved. Its narrow full-component errors fall **18.292/9.450 → 4.787/4.601**, but spill fractions **increase**, and core-average covariance worsens **.248 → .289**. Lower covariance cannot be described as eliminating stray events. Gaussian stationary checks fall **33/72 → 28/72**, shifted hold **12/24 → 11/24**, and deadline reacquisition still fails. Final KS **.06065 > .05** despite passing individual mean/width bounds. Keep this as an opt-in scoped repair, with explicit continuous-learning and width regressions.

## Why the other approaches still fail

Hydraulic tests a contraction preference as well as bounded travel. Its uncensored saved-center Jacobian variance proxy is still too broad: median **4.456** times target total variance, versus **2.976** for the matched winner. This first-order proxy omits nonlinear activation crossings and is not a served-law covariance score. Actual native precision improves to **.50133**, but covariance bias worsens **+.37138 → +.90183** and radial KS remains **.33394**. Center RMS **.36362 sigma** describes only the scorer's within-radius population, not all outputs. A network-only secant penalty scaled by global real variance supplies neither the correct local target width nor a joint finite-deformation constraint. Underweighting local density, compensation and normalized response remain competing explanations, not established causal findings. The successor fixes finite zero-spacing handling before D steps; no finite-atom/image/conditional training qualification follows.

Corrected projection resolves the implementation question. Inactive Gaussian and two-pole parameter states, base optimizer states, named streams and scored outputs now match the control bitwise. Conditional protected derivatives after actual rounded updates are below **1.2e-8**, yet the wrong trajectory row cycle **2 → 5 → 8 → 11 → 2** remains. Adversarial nonascent supplies no per-row identity guarantee; residual already protects an informative paired loss and also fails. Thus missing paired information alone cannot explain both tasks. Useful strict progress, finite-step curvature and identity ambiguity remain distinct unresolved questions. The old schema1 measurements remain confounded evidence under their original identity.

Blacksmith's coherence gain is bounded, but bounds alone do not preserve useful learning. Saved Gaussian gains average **.299 G / .090 D / .078 prior**, at each tensor/row's last observed update. One global rule therefore changes player balance and can suppress useful stochastic signal. The broad-vector covariance/eigen failures and loss of smoke support rejecting this exact all-player .95 rule. The experiment does not isolate which role caused the regression, and its own Gaussian stability is genuinely unmeasured after smoke FAIL.

Alchemy shows why a surrogate forecast can succeed while the task collapses. Its unequal-width covariance average **.6098 ≤ .85** coexists with empty modes and a zero minimum eigen ratio. The full mass/variance gates reject that result. A separate saved-output diagnostic also finds the witness's own occupancy residual worsens while its shape residual improves; its mass gradient is nonzero. Weak distant-cell gradients, changing minibatch frames, adversarial conflict and normalized shared-network response are plausible explanations, but these output-space probes do not establish a causal parameter-space mechanism. The soft-cell loss is a finite-feature discrepancy, not a general guarantee that mass, shape and coverage will jointly improve.

## Recommendations and scope

1. **Prioritize transport's demonstrated rare-density repair.** Inspect remaining spill distances and local forces, then freeze a separately bounded transfer or successor question. Do not call it a global winner or silently combine it with hydraulic.
2. **Keep hydraulic travel as a useful lead; stop this exact secant package.** A future deformation proposal should distinguish mean transport from local shape, use relevant training evidence and demonstrate that its applied joint response controls the failure it targets.
3. **Move beyond unchanged nonascent projection.** Its numerical guarantee is now tested cleanly. A future hypothesis must address useful progress or the existing identity-bearing signal, while respecting the original supervision contract.
4. **Stop the exact moonshots.** Preserve their equations, source and failures. Any successor needs new evidence and a substantive declared mechanism rather than renamed coefficient sweeps or seed repeats.
