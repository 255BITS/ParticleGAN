# Conditional integration, round five

This preregistered four-arm diagnostic tests whether one global direction-only
blend plus exact local-v2 transport retains both conditional identity and rare
and broad density repairs. It adds explicit output-marginal consumers to three
source-bound variants; the originals remain unsupported for transport.

The four arms are the exact BCAP winner, exact PR367 direction blend, exact
local-v2, and direction blend plus local-v2. The strict-progress optimizer and
local-v2 implementation are unchanged. All arms share the same frozen variants,
seed 0, public deterministic initialization, architecture, target/data law,
actual batches, prior, sampling, full update allowance and evaluation cadence.

Trajectory and residual compare their 12 generated fast arcs with the existing
12 fast targets; slow arcs are checked conditioning inputs, excluded from the
transport distance. Trajectory gains no paired-MSE supervision. Residual retains
its existing both-land paired loss. Mid-scale compares one output per original
training scale (-1,0,.5,1) with the corresponding teacher panel; the scale labels
are excluded from distance. Its original paired cover and identity/concept gates
remain unchanged. These marginal losses admit output-row permutations. Their
scalar value therefore cannot establish temporal or conditional correctness.

Transport adds the original empirical sliced quadratic loss (32 fixed directions)
and relative real-anchor local feature loss, each global weight 1. It adds no
labels, evaluator parameters, RNG draws or serving changes. Direction protects
only the original adversarial and active paired losses, excluding transport.
[Gretton et al.](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v13/gretton12a.html) and
[Li et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v37/li15.html) support kernel moment
matching and differentiable generator training. [Sener and Koltun](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.04650)
provide multiobjective context; none establishes these GAN retention gates.

[Saved evidence](prior-evidence.json) was inspected before preregistration.
[Preregistration](preregistration.json) freezes 28 cells and full reservation
38,880 seconds. The campaign cap is 42,000; ancillary checks reserve 1,200;
total track ceiling 43,200. Two-pole is excluded and remains BLOCKED under its
original transport contract. Gaussian own-smoke dependencies remain mandatory.
Complete original sustained identity, density and retention gates decide each
result; endpoint improvements do not replace them. Any failing conditional,
rare or broad gate falsifies the retained union; Gaussian retention failure
blocks a global repair. There is no sweep, additional candidate, seed study,
continuation, ordinary qualification or default promotion.
