## Exact mechanism and explicit consumers

All arms retain the exact winning nonsaturating BCAP recipe: full DualNorm, momentum 0, smoothing .001, per-offset layout, G/E .012, D .018, prior .030, constant floors 1, BCAP coefficient 1/cap 1/every update, no additive training noise, EMA or extra scalar prior regularization. The deltas are only `constraint_geometry_mode` (none or exact PR367 `direction_blend`) and the two exact local-v2 weights (0 or1), with 32 deterministic directions. Strict-progress and local-v2 module bytes remain unchanged. Direction uses the same full common-descent blend as PR367, without finite probes; transport is added to the total objective and is never a new protected loss.

The new public `OutputMarginalTransport` consumer adds the existing empirical sliced-W2 and relative local real-anchor feature losses. Real targets, anchors, fourth-neighbor widths and normalizers are detached. Gradients flow from generated outputs to the original network and learned prior locations. Conditioning is checked but excluded from the transport distance; no oracle evaluator geometry, labels, new draw, task-specific weight or serving change enters. Zero weights return the original loss tensor.

These are output-marginal losses: a row-permuted target admits zero transport loss while failing identity. [Saved endpoint probes](prior-evidence.json) and destructive controls retain that distinction. Marginal set matching cannot silently replace temporal or conditional correctness. The consumers reuse existing G-phase tensors; they do not add real target draws or generator forwards. Mid-scale retains its original explicit zero-origin parameter fixture and public KEEP initializer policy, with no sampled prior; it is a separate host cohort from the learned conditional networks. Trajectory/residual enumerate learned cloud locations under their original explicit exceptions. Scalar/vector hosts retain positive-width learned MoG with fixed widths and uniform weights.

All four arms use the same variants. Architecture, data/targets, actual seen batches, priors, initializer, sampling, committed-update budget, original evaluation cadence and full thresholds remain fixed. Each variant retains its original question ID. The three original cards remain unchanged and transport-bearing original contracts remain BLOCKED. Two-pole is excluded and has no implicit consumer. Native, unequal width, images and words are unmeasured.

The exact direction and local-v2 implementations come from independently archived PR367/368 evidence, inspected before registration. [Gretton et al.](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v13/gretton12a.html) provide MMD theory and [Li et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v37/li15.html) show differentiable generator moment matching; this finite data-dependent feature loss has no characteristic-population guarantee. [Sener and Koltun](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.04650) provide multiobjective context. The analogies and frozen-batch first-order property do not prove stochastic GAN density, finite descent or retention convergence.
