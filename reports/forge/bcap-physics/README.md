The hydraulic experiment provides the clearest evidence that useful direction and useful travel are different. Native proposals average **.245865** output travel before limiting and **.008405** after it; all 7,000 updates satisfy the measured bound. Precision approximately doubles, but remains far below **.97**. Its reported native center RMS is computed only among within-radius samples, as specified by the [accuracy evaluator](../../../benchmarks/toy100/accuracy.py); it cannot establish correct placement of the entire served population. A separate deterministic all-20,000-center Jacobian diagnostic finds median local MoG variance/target variance **2.976 → 4.618**. Thus the measured location/precision gains coexist with worse local linearized kernel width. Bounding sampled output displacement does not itself bound local covariance or unseen-neighborhood deformation. The experiment does not identify which network/prior interaction causes that widening.

The hydraulic Gaussian misses are specific. Update **3,959** has target KS **.06810**, and shifted update **5,125** has **.05402**, against **.05**. Their moment-matched normal KS values are **.01828** and **.00791**. Mean/width stay inside their individual bounds; the target CDF gate is stricter than those moment bounds. These observations do not support attributing the misses solely to severe nonnormal shape. The exact radius also requires positive-spacing batches: repeated noiseless examples can give zero median spacing, which raises after D has already stepped. Image, conditional and finite-atom transfer are untested.

Transport makes the distinction between mass allocation and local density concrete. Unequal-width center counts move from **95/13/108/40** to **62/63/65/66**, while full covariance error worsens **6.28756 → 7.03857**. Two narrow components have full errors **18.2922/9.4500**, despite core-only errors **.3889/.1919**. Boundary spill remains material. Unequal mass reaches its allocation prediction, but variance failures at **1,050 and 1,100** break the five-check terminal suffix. Rare-mode training evidence is also intermittent: the component is absent from **85/1,200** fixed batches. That absence is measured; its causal contribution to the failed variance checks is unresolved.

The other theories clarify what their guarantees leave open. Optimism damps a scoped small-step bilinear rotation, while this experiment uses nonlinear normalized, alternating networks and an unchanged moving prior; the measured regression does not falsify every circulation-aware method. Spectral-half preserves an algebraic cap and orthogonal equivariance, while reducing weaker singular motion and total Frobenius motion. Lower blob background error accompanies much larger foreground error, consistent with losing useful signal. Its gradient Gram surrogate does not estimate Fisher information or loss curvature. Projection constrains existing loss derivatives, while trajectory has no paired-MSE training term to protect; first-order non-ascent also need not supply sufficient progress. Furthermore, the trained projection implementation recomposes parameters even when unchanged, allowing float32 rounding drift. Gaussian smoke fails with **0/1,000** projections. Schema2 fixes the no-op path in software; all scientific measurements remain bound to the original schema1 source.

Recommended follow-ups, before another training round:

1. **Continue the output-motion theory through saved-state analysis.** Separate mean drift, covariance deformation and network/prior compensation; define support for zero-spacing laws before a new global candidate. Preserve the exact failed strict gates and the broad-vector guardrail.
2. **Continue transport as a distinct allocation mechanism.** Inspect saved signal balance and core/spill behavior. Allocation gains do not authorize changing prior widths, mixture weights, sampling laws or numerical gates.
3. **Resolve the projection implementation question.** The corrected no-op implementation has software checks and no scientific training result. A future comparison must use the corrected source on both arms; the current conditional differences do not isolate projection.
4. **Stop the exact optimism and spectral-half revisions.** Existing regressions support no further unchanged run, seed repeat or automatic continuation. An actual-curvature idea needs a different declared metric and mechanism, rather than relabeling the tested gradient spectrum.

These are priorities for a separately bounded program, not new admitted experiments. Bars, anisotropic/overlap vectors, rotated/staggered native tasks, and the original word/ring/other passing gates were not comprehensively remeasured here. No combination of task-specific successes supplies a new global winner, and composing hydraulic and transport would require its own matched study.
