## A combined recipe preserves the measured repairs

The four-arm integration isolates the useful roles. Local-v2 alone fails both conditional identities; direction alone fails rare density. BOTH passes all three explicit conditional variants, unequal mass, broad and Gaussian smoke, while failing Gaussian retention. Trajectory MSE is **.000236766**, residual MSE **.000222020**, residual own-pad success **1** and wrong-pad rate **0**. The identity MSE bound remains **.02**, with five complete terminal passes. Passing suffixes are **18 / 20 / 21** for trajectory, residual and mid-scale.

Rare full covariance improves **.522577 → .386670** versus local-v2, and the passing suffix extends **5 → 9**. Mass TV slightly worsens **.016426 → .018623** within its bound. Broad remains PASS, although covariance worsens **.226564 → .331816** and suffix shortens **24 → 9**. The combined configuration preserves these repairs without dominating every metric.

The public `OutputMarginalTransport` consumer adds the unchanged sliced-W2 and local real-anchor losses to existing G-phase output tensors. Original slow arcs or scales remain conditioning inputs and are excluded from the transport distance. Targets, anchors and normalizers are detached; gradients reach the original network and learned prior locations. No new draw, forward, evaluator geometry or paired supervision enters. Trajectory still protects its original adversarial signal; residual and mid-scale retain their existing paired signals. Transport is part of the total objective, never a new protected loss.

All four arms use the same explicit trajectory/residual/mid-scale variants, retaining original architecture, targets, initialization, priors, batches, budget, cadence and full gates. Their source declarations name the new consumer and preserve original question IDs. A row-permuted target can have zero marginal transport loss while failing identity, so the original identity objectives and scorers remain essential. The audit verifies active transport on **400 / 400 / 800** conditional updates and **10 / 30 / 9** direction conflicts, with zero Pareto stalls. This is a measured union under that consumer law, not evidence for every conditional input or a native/image transfer.

The three-arm regression study confirms the scalar/vector portion of the integration result on a separate frozen source. Each arm retains rare and broad PASSes; every Gaussian retention and unequal-width gate fails.

Direction's final KS satisfies the **.05** bound but cannot replace the required **72 stationary checks**, deadline reacquisition and **24 shifted-hold checks**. Removing finite acceptance produces a different path but does not recover continuous learning. This rules out the simple claim that the finite layer alone causes the regression. It does not uniquely identify the competing adversarial/empirical-transport fields or the historical updates responsible for failure. Balanced assignment and prior-only routing also yield individually good Gaussian endpoints while their full retention gates fail.

## Allocation and shape require separate explanations

Balanced assignment solves a detached squared-distance bijection inside each contiguous **128-row block**, then differentiates the selected Torch costs. Every row participates once. It retains local-v2's local features, global/local weight 1, prior width and uniform masses. Native batches use 16 blocks; vector/Gaussian batches use one. This is conservative empirical transport within blocks, not full-batch transport or a population guarantee.

Unequal width gains a real sustained repair: **14/24** complete checks pass, with terminal suffix **14**, full covariance **.286062**, mass TV **.015625** and all four components populated. Narrow center-output covariance traces fall **5.1229 / 4.3290 → .7219 / .8891** times target trace. These exact `G(location)` endpoint populations use diagnostic assignments and exclude nonlinear kernel jitter; the served-law sustained PASS determines the result.

Rare allocation improves while shape deteriorates. Center-population TV falls **.005000 → .001563**, with counts **141/77/32/6 → 141/77/33/5**, yet the rare center trace rises **.7757 → 3.7285** times target trace. Served full covariance worsens **.522577 → 1.099564** and the terminal suffix becomes **0**. This is a spread/retention failure despite preserved rare occupancy. Finite minibatch mass fluctuations and the shared map's response remain plausible explanations; the endpoint census does not attribute the whole training history.

Native makes the same distinction especially clear. Both 100,000-draw holdouts occupy all 100 nearest cells, and assignment reduces mass TV **.08646 → .08002** and uncensored covariance RMS **35.3650 → 9.0178**. Nevertheless precision falls **.21973 → .15276**, genuine-quality modes fall **17 → 9**, and spill rises **.78027 → .84724**. Minimum genuine mass becomes zero. A lower covariance scalar or conserved output coupling therefore cannot certify native density. These holdout quantities remain separate from the scheduled 20,000-draw diagnostic cohort.

## Center spread and force coupling explain different limits

The component census exhaustively enumerates learned centers and estimates nonlinear conditional kernel moments using Gauss-Hermite quadrature. For local-v2's unequal-width narrow components, center-output trace ratios **5.122888 / 4.328984** dwarf conditional-jitter ratios **.057369 / .020522**. Between-kernel conditional means account for **99.317%** of average fixed-assignment covariance trace. Native's corresponding fraction is only **44.625%**. Fixed assignments and approximate Gaussian integration limit these figures; they support different contributor hypotheses across tasks.

Restricting transport gradients to prior locations improves the narrow center clouds but increases their conditional jitter contributions. Width's final covariance **.843668** meets the endpoint bound, yet only one terminal check passes. Rare covariance worsens to **1.361399** with suffix zero. Dominant center spread therefore does not imply that removing G's transport gradient is the right control. The latent pullback still depends on the shared network, and the adversarial gradient still moves that network.

Saved force probes trace output gradients through shared parameters, FullDualNorm and actual finite motion. Native rowwise uphill fractions are **5.08%** for the raw shared-gradient displacement, **3.61%** after normalization and **3.76%** for finite motion; the vector probe gives **30.18% / 21.19% / 21.29%**. Normalization improves some measured alignment, so the evidence does not support describing it as uniformly harmful. Conditional independently normalized proposals remain strongly nonadditive, with ratios **.996914 / 1.215825**. These are endpoint probes, not a unique historical loss attribution.

The candidate filters sample forces through a Rayleigh-damped empirical `J Jᵀ` system, with at most four matrix-free conjugate-gradient iterations, before the existing optimizer and direction repair. It preserves conditional PASSes but only raises native precision to **.28345** and genuine-quality modes **8 → 16**. Full native and Gaussian gates still fail. Maximum relative solver residual reaches **.9900** native and **2.4554** Gaussian, so the result rejects this specific approximation/damping/downstream package, not all possible function-space methods.

## Recommendations

1. **Keep direction plus local-v2 as the combined reference for the measured conditional and rare/broad scope.** Preserve the exact consumer law and identity gates. Its Gaussian retention failure and unequal-width regression remain part of that configuration; native composition is unmeasured.
2. **Keep balanced assignment as a width-specific experimental reference.** It adds a complete sustained repair, but its rare and native regressions prohibit global replacement. Examine the saved narrow and rare center populations together before another allocation/shape mechanism.
3. **Focus the next investigation on Gaussian temporal failure.** The finite-layer ablation has answered its bounded question. Inspect saved adversarial and empirical-transport fields across acquisition, stationary failure and target shift before choosing a new intervention. Good final KS, local loss decrease and acquisition are insufficient.
4. **Stop the exact prior-only routing and CG4 force-filter packages as global candidates.** Preserve their contributor and force diagnostics. A later mechanism needs a distinct supported hypothesis and bounded registration rather than another unchanged run or seed variation.

These priorities do not launch another campaign. No merge, public-default change or ordinary qualification follows. The implementations remain in their five PRs; this parent publication links and audits their evidence.
