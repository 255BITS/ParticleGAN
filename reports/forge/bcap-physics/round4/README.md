# BCAP repair research round four

**The common-descent direction blend alone preserves both conditional identity repairs.** Finite acceptance is unnecessary for those measured gates. Projection plus transport local-v2 preserves the rare-density repair on supported vector hosts, with a longer passing suffix, but worsens Gaussian retention and cannot yet run on the conditional hosts. Actual native overshoot is now measured: reducing it improves precision without repairing mass balance, covariance or tails. The tail-moment successor collapses modes; balancing generator and prior motion improves some geometry but creates no complete sustained repair.

These five completed studies answer the [round-three priorities](../round3/README.md) with three-arm projection ablation, two matched mechanism comparisons against the exact BCAP winner, a transport successor against local-v2, and a four-arm composition comparison. Each arm uses one global trainer configuration, protocol seed 0, unchanged task laws and complete original numerical gates. These are bounded diagnostics under provisional profiles. The original winner retains its archived **7/21 Tier 2** result in the [single current technique inventory](../../technique-inventory.md); specialist results do not add passes to that configuration.

## Direction is sufficient for the measured conditional repairs

Trajectory MSE remains **.000258480** with or without finite acceptance, versus corrected nonascent **.243736356**; its observed terminal passing suffix is **19**. Residual MSE is **.000249089** for direction alone and **.000369592** with finite checks, versus **.062639892** for nonascent. Both repaired residual arms correctly place every row, have zero wrong-pad rate and retain suffix **21**. The MSE bound remains **.02**, with five complete terminal passes required. Mid-scale also retains its full PASS and suffix **20**.

All 12 trajectory conflicts accept full scale in the finite arm. Removing finite checks leaves its scored trajectory and final numerical state bitwise identical. Residual takes a different path: direction alone has 55 conflicts, while the finite arm has 186 and needs 625 reductions, down to scale 1/64. Both nevertheless retain the full identity gates. This isolates the finite-check layer: it changes local motion and supplies a same-batch protected-loss certificate, but is unnecessary for these measured task passes. The ablation does not separate the feasible-projection half from the common-descent half of the direction, establish population convergence, or test broad native/image transfer.

Saved endpoint attribution strengthens the earlier coverage-versus-identity hypothesis while retaining its limits. At the original trajectory endpoint, the normalized combined proposal has identity derivative **+.005789**; removing set coverage changes it to **−.020440**. Removing adversarial loss instead gives **+.019850**. At the original residual endpoint, removing the existing paired signal reverses the derivative from **−.000426** to **+.003083**. Coverage is the largest observed antagonist at those endpoints, and the existing adversarial/paired signals help. These counterfactual endpoint derivatives do not identify the terms responsible for earlier conflict events or justify removing coverage globally.

Full DualNorm is nonlinear: independently normalized loss proposals do not add to the normalized combined proposal. The reported nonadditivity ratios are **.99691** and **1.21582**. Every saved endpoint already has negative combined protected derivatives, including failing endpoints. Loss attribution, normalization and training history therefore remain distinct questions. Trajectory still receives no new paired-MSE supervision; residual retains only its existing informative paired loss.

## Native finite overshoot is real but does not explain the whole density failure

The native winner's G/prior objective is adversarial only, with prior regularization zero. It has no required latent-to-mode identity or separate set-coverage loss. Conditional objective conflict is therefore not the native explanation.

An exact-source reversible next-update probe replaces the earlier linearized-motion inference with actual finite movement. Its full proposal travels a median **7.607 target sigmas** and decreases batch loss slightly, but fails sufficient decrease; diagnostic precision falls **.23970 → .23435**. Half scale gives **.61550** in that discarded probe. Oracle geometry is used only for diagnostic evaluation, never the candidate's training rule, and all model/optimizer/buffer/RNG state is restored.

During the new native candidate's 7,000 updates, every proposal is locally downhill. Yet **6,884** full proposals increase finite batch loss, and another two decrease it insufficiently. The controller shrinks **6,886** proposals, accepts every update with zero Armijo violation and has mean accepted scale **.226304**. A first-order-conflict-only trigger would miss these finite failures. Useful critic direction and locally downhill parameter motion can coexist with excessive finite travel.

The resulting precision improves to **.65938**, and modes with genuine quality mass of at least .005 increase **8 → 63**, but all 100 are required. Minimum genuine mass is **.001740**, mass TV **.14892**, and all five terminal native checks still fail. Full-cell covariance Frobenius RMS worsens **62.9694 → 87.2746** even though spill falls **.75928 → .34062**. In-radius moments and uncensored moments describe different portions of the law. Finite loss descent repairs neither automatically.

Generator/prior decomposition provides a second measured view. In the original native endpoint probe, network/prior/joint RMS travel is **.245671 / .036805 / .268698**, with nonlinear cross RMS **.002467**. Network center travel **.245057** greatly exceeds jitter deformation **.005016**. These are one next-update cohort, not an attribution of the entire training history. A three-row transport tail probe likewise finds larger outward network motion, with a correspondingly narrow scope.

The role-balance candidate enforces its finite travel bound on every native update and improves precision to **.63726**, but mass TV worsens **.14718 → .18736**. Gaussian final KS reaches **.045352**, while stationary passes **62/72**, shifted hold **13/24** and failed deadline reacquisition leave the full retention verdict FAIL. Unequal-width core covariance improves **.444178 → .296830**, but full covariance worsens **6.287559 → 11.472665**. Its saved-center census finds narrow-component center covariance traces **15.30 / 21.34** times target trace, versus linearized kernel contributions **.0626 / .0208**. The census uses fixed assignments and a local approximation; actual served scores remain authoritative. It supports separating center-population spread from within-kernel deformation before another controller, rather than assuming that small Jacobian spread means correct density.

## Composition preserves rare density while exposing host and retention limits

The four-arm comparison reproduces both singleton mechanisms on one source. Projection alone retains both conditional PASSes; local-v2 retains unequal-mass and broad PASSes. BOTH adds transport to the total generator/prior objective while protecting the same existing adversarial loss on scalar hosts. Transport is not silently added as a second protected loss. BOTH uses strict finite projection; a composition using the simpler direction-only repair remains a separate question.

On unequal mass, BOTH reduces full covariance **.522577 → .350241**, improves the minimum eigen ratio **.366567 → .581130**, reduces rare spill **.140351 → .126984**, and extends the passing suffix **5 → 9**. Mass TV slightly worsens **.016426 → .018867** within the passing bound. Broad remains PASS. Projection is active, including 66 unequal-mass conflicts and 199 broad conflicts; the gain is not an inactive combination.

Gaussian is the tradeoff: stationary passes fall **28/72 → 8/72**, shifted hold **11/24 → 1/24**, and final KS rises **.060653 → .241544** relative to local-v2. BOTH therefore preserves the rare repair without becoming a global replacement. Its conditional, mid-scale and two-pole hosts do not consume the transport sample-space package, yielding four BLOCKED cells per transport-bearing arm. This comparison establishes a supported vector composition, not the union of conditional and rare-density repairs.

The separate tail-moment successor illustrates why a favorable scalar cannot replace the full gates. Unequal-width covariance falls **2.480438 → .822211**, but three modes disappear; their missing-component penalties contribute error 1 each. All 4,096 draws occupy the fourth component and mass TV becomes **.75**. Unequal mass loses every rare draw and the broad mixture loses one mode. The unbounded radial features address an escaping-tail limitation, yet neither their mathematical growth nor same-batch loss decrease ensures correct allocation. Rational partition leakage, batch anchors, global scale and shared-map deformation remain competing explanations for this collapse. Preserve local-v2 and stop this exact successor.

## Recommended next questions

1. **Preserve direction blend as the simplest measured conditional repair.** Keep strict finite acceptance available for its local certificate. Further component isolation or transfer should retain both identity gates and the mid-scale guardrail; no new identity supervision is justified by this round.
2. **Resolve the public host interface before claiming a combined repair.** Declare exactly how conditional hosts consume transport, including the target/conditioning law and protected-loss selection, then compare the same singleton and combined mechanisms under unchanged baseline task conditions. The current BLOCKED cells are an implementation boundary, not evidence that conditional composition succeeds or fails scientifically.
3. **Investigate center-population allocation and tails separately from jitter width.** Use saved finite role decompositions and uncensored per-component density diagnostics to choose a data-driven mechanism that preserves local-v2's rare and broad PASSes. Neither finite descent, RMS balance, lower core covariance nor a lower averaged covariance scalar is sufficient. Track genuine quality mass, missing modes, full covariance, spill and temporal retention together.

These are priorities for a future bounded study, not an automatic new sweep or continuation. Retain BOTH as a vector lead with a Gaussian regression; retain native finite-overshoot and role-attribution evidence as explanations and partial improvements. Stop the exact tail-moment, native Armijo and role-balance packages as global repair candidates. No merge, default change or ordinary qualification follows.
