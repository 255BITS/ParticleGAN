## What the conditional repair establishes

Trajectory's unchanged identity MSE bound is **.02**, and residual also requires every row correct, zero wrong-pad rate and five terminal full passes. The new final values are **.000258480** and **.000369592**. The matched control reproduces the prior failures at **.243736356** and **.062639892**. Trajectory passes **19/24** checks with terminal suffix **19**; residual passes **21/24**, suffix **21**, versus **0/24** and suffix0 for both controls. The four-row wrong trajectory cycle resolves, and every saved endpoint row is nearest its own target in both successful candidates.

The change addresses two gaps in boundary-only projection: a feasible update need not make strict progress, and a negative local derivative need not survive finite movement. On originally conflicting steps, the candidate adds a bounded common-descent component, then accepts the actual rounded update only when the existing protected losses decrease sufficiently on the same fixed batch. It draws no extra training samples and advances the base optimizer/RNG clocks once. Trajectory retains its adversarial protected loss without a new paired-MSE term; residual retains its already available paired signal. The successful trajectory therefore shows that its existing signal can support identity learning under this update package. Missing explicit paired supervision is not a sufficient account of the previous failure.

The experiment changes both direction and finite acceptance together. It does not isolate which ingredient is necessary or prove population/general game convergence. Gaussian protection is inactive and its complete retention FAIL is unchanged. All **243** conflict updates accept strict finite common descent, with zero trained stalls or rejections. Trajectory accepts all **12** at full scale, with no backtracking; residual accepts **186**, requiring **625** reductions and a smallest scale **1/64**. This supports restored descent direction as the trajectory lead and demonstrates finite realization operationally in residual, while preserving the package-level causal limitation. The conditional success, including its particle-cloud exception and fixed task laws, grants no learned-MoG, image, native or full-suite qualification by substitution.

## Why the other numerical guarantees remain insufficient

**Transport realizes batch descent but loses sustained density.** Its saved-state probes showed negative directional derivatives coexisting with actual loss increases; halving the same proposal restored descent. The admitted controller then satisfies the recorded finite decrease bound for all **9,600** candidate updates, contracts **1,517** proposals and rejects none. Nevertheless, the preceding unequal-mass PASS becomes FAIL. Rare centers increase **6 → 7**, but rare spill rises **.140351 → .269841** and rare full covariance **.470663 → 2.986301**. At update1,050 the minimum eigen ratio falls to **.130087 < .15**; the endpoint full covariance also fails. Width core covariance improves **.289171 → .179413** while full covariance worsens **2.480438 → 3.859733**. Final Gaussian KS worsens **.060653 → .082014**. Broad proposals all accept full scale and their complete trajectory/states match the control bitwise. A same-batch changing-critic objective is not a guarantee of local covariance, rare-mode fidelity or future distribution retention. The comparison identifies a harmful consequence of this package under the declared law; it does not establish which term or rejected portion of useful motion caused it. Preserve local-v2's earlier rare-density repair instead of replacing it with this successor.

**Hydraulic controls sampled shape rather than the whole density law.** Real-batch neighborhood capacities replace the earlier global antithetic contraction penalty. The controller preserves midpoint mean to first order while correcting excess response, then checks finite travel and sampled shape. Its inferred graph is a data-dependent estimate, not a target-mixture oracle, and its guarantees cover the tested rows/jitters. All **9,200** candidate updates satisfy the recorded finite shape/travel bounds with zero ray rejection, yet the complete Gaussian and native tasks fail. Gaussian ends at KS **.999971**, mean error **4.409506 sigma** and width ratio **.071406**, leaving its own dependent stability BLOCKED. Native holdout precision is **.03876**, and per-mode accuracy covariance fields remain **null** because there is insufficient evidence; null does not mean zero covariance error. Its uncensored saved-center Jacobian variance proxy falls to about **.259** times target total variance, meeting an upper-bound forecast through contraction alongside severe coverage loss. This proxy is a separate first-order diagnostic, not served-law variance. Gaussian graphs average **35.891** neighborhoods for a unimodal target; native graphs average **134.581**, and the native mean ray scale is only **.02864**. Fragmented empirical frames, suppressed useful motion and nonlinear mean drift are plausible competing explanations, rather than established causes. Shape/travel feasibility supplies no general mass-allocation or adversarial-progress guarantee. The exact experiment is stopped with its source and failures preserved.

**Fresh game reevaluation repairs some late geometry but regresses retention.** Unequal width passes every endpoint bound and its last four scheduled observations; update **1,000** fails only quality, **.801025 < .85**, breaking the required five-check suffix. No extra update or altered suffix turns that into a PASS. Gaussian stationary checks improve **2/72 → 20/72**, shifted hold **0/24 → 6/24**, but deadline reacquisition fails and final KS **.058208 > .05**. Broad-vector PASS is retained. Mode hold's final **5/8** modes and **.220459** quality show that a scoped bilinear damping calculation does not establish useful nonlinear normalized G/D/prior dynamics. The saved field-response probes motivate reevaluation but do not measure a rotational Jacobian or identify the role responsible for collapse.

**Characteristic identification does not protect finite-discrepancy tails.** The kernel candidate's unequal-width four-sigma core covariance improves **.444178 → .259211**, while full covariance worsens **6.287559 → 13.431491**. All four modes remain populated, but the narrowest component's spill rises **0 → .109290** and its full covariance error reaches **44.654877**. A separate endpoint diagnostic finds the empirical witness decreases **.087147 → .029458** and aligns with the critic in G/prior parameter space. Absent attraction or persistent endpoint critic opposition is therefore unsupported by that probe. A bounded kernel can make discrepancy small for a small contaminated mass while far-away contamination makes variance arbitrarily large; this explains a mathematical limitation, not the precise trajectory cause. Adaptive batch scales, shared-network deformation and normalized finite motion remain competing mechanisms. Gaussian improves to **40/72** stationary and **17/24** shifted checks with reacquisition PASS, but final KS **.070755** and the complete retention gate still fail.

## Recommendations and evidence scope

1. **Prioritize projection's demonstrated conditional repair for the next separately bounded question.** Test transfer or isolate direction versus finite acceptance without adding supervision, changing gates or rerunning this unchanged study merely for a merge. It is the strongest new result, with Gaussian instability still unresolved.
2. **Retain transport local-v2 as the scoped rare-density repair.** Stop the backtracking replacement. Inspect saved rare-component dynamics and tail-sensitive applied forces before another substantive width/retention proposal.
3. **Preserve game correction's late unequal-width result as a lead, not a repaired task.** A future question must address its mode-hold regression and failed earlier quality, not append an unchanged continuation to obtain a passing suffix.
4. **Stop the exact hydraulic and kernel global revisions.** Keep their equations, actual controller/witness measurements and negative results. Local empirical shape bounds and lower bounded-kernel discrepancy are insufficient substitutes for complete mass, tail, covariance and temporal gates.
