## Phase 1 Parallel diagnosis

Three isolated workflows investigated two-pole causality, word performance, and compatibility. Two-pole has at most six original-contract diagnostic arms, with 1,800 seconds of full reservations. Profiling and targeted software verification share a separate 1,200-second allowance. Short profiling fixtures cannot certify a training gate. All bulk artifacts live under `/mnt/ml7tb/ParticleGAN-forge/bcap-tier1-repair-next`.

Completed diagnosis: projection alone exactly reproduces the incumbent's two-pole path, while global transport is sufficient to reproduce the combination's short passing suffix. The incumbent has 17 consecutive terminal passes; transport and the combination have one, below the original requirement of five. Local-only transport has a late slope excursion. This fixture therefore points to transport changing the critic's operating path, rather than a projection conflict. [Certified ablations and saved-state audit](../bcap-tier1-stability/README.md).

Two preregistered repairs pass that screen. A constant training cap of 0.9 has nine consecutive terminal passes; a same-training-panel finite critic-step guard has seventeen. The latter rejects 49 of 80 critic displacements, so its better scoped slope bound may trade away learning on larger hosts. Neither short screen establishes full Tier 1 performance.

Full SVD accounts for 89.7% of CUDA self time in the bounded word profile. The optional CPU full-SVD backend reduces measured update time from 43.774 to 17.635 milliseconds, a 2.48× profile speedup. Its projected 353-second training time excludes full-run evaluation and checkpoint overhead. CUDA and CPU SVD rounding changes the actual training path; it is an explicit numerical trainer change requiring fresh gate measurements. Native defaults match the archived source bitwise in a separate matched replay. [Profiles, implementation and limitations](../bcap-tier1-performance/README.md).

Fresh integrated compatibility checks pass for both compound repairs: 78 checks each, including 42 exact state pairs against the actual frozen `develop` source. All previous comparators and receipts are preserved; an outside-Git adapter adds precisely two checked inactive metadata defaults. Source inventories include the new optimizer and recipe files. The failure modes separate into several mechanisms. First, allocation and aggregate geometry can improve without full local density. Global covariance includes the separation between mode centers; full component covariance measures every assigned point, including tails. A core-only statistic discards tails. Secant's ring has global error .098413 but full component error 57.418460 and HQ .423828. Confidence's unequal-width core error .351611 coexists with full error 10.812900, despite better mass allocation. These observations favor separating coverage, allocation, core shape and spill in the next density investigation; they do not justify replacing a failed gate with a nicer diagnostic.

Second, geometry activation does not establish useful force. Anisotropic ridge/neighbor counters prove the kernel was used, but its anisotropic prediction fails at component error 2.078374. Its constant-target case has degenerate local covariance and retains identity while the auxiliary loss can be nearly constant; actual gradient norms were not saved. Entropic transport's constant panel has zero real variance, so the float32 variance floor multiplies coordinate-mean squared error by 4,194,304, matching maximum loss 2,097,152. Zero marginal residual on that panel does not validate these force units. Other finite-round residual maxima reach 2.211722; the truncated solver is not evidence of exact optimal transport. This scaling is a plausible contributor to unused-token drift, with causation between the objective and CPU backend unresolved by the compound intervention.

Third, confidence and secant change response strength without certifying the proposed physics. Confidence's anisotropic mobility mean is .05827 and often zero, but this is a loss multiplier before a normalized optimizer, not an accepted-motion fraction or calibrated probability. On the unused-token panel its mobility is one throughout and retention fails. Secant's ring mean fractions are .09420/.09423/.07357 for G/D/prior, with the floor used on 81.5–87.8% of calls; native network floors exceed 95%. Strong damping is measured, while improved curvature estimation is not established. Noise, opponent motion and sparse revisit intervals can enter gradient differences. Optimism's endpoint ring thresholds pass but its suffix is two rather than five, and Gaussian/word failure blocks its own continuations. Across these ideas, a transient endpoint or active controller is insufficient evidence of sustained repair.

Direction-only projection supplies the narrower positive result. Trajectory records **12 blended steps out of 400**, residual student **55 out of 400**, and neither records a stall. Mean retained displacement norm on conflicting steps is .70179/.78094. The stored positive derivative maximum after blending is zero; that statistic is floored at zero and is not a finite-step loss-descent proof. The owner also verifies exact model/prior tensor bytes and zero activation for eleven intended inactive task families. Together with all sixteen matched consumed streams and the two repaired original gates, this supports an intervention on existing loss conflicts. It does not show a universal optimizer, improved critic dynamics, or a solution to the remaining marginal-density tasks.
