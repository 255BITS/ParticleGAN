## What improved and what still fails

Removing LR, beta2 and penalty annealing together repairs external-clock dependence for every tested constant policy. Their own clock receipts pass the source audit and step-label, horizon, cadence and restart state comparisons. This structural change does not repair local density.

No Ring16 observation in any of the ten distinct whole recipes meets the unchanged component covariance bound of 0.85. Every arm has 0/96 full passes and a terminal passing suffix of 0/5. The three lower-rate children preserve their parents' complete policies except for global LR 0.0085 to 0.00425:

The lower-rate beta2=0.99/gamma=0.1 policy gives the best observed covariance error, 1.409079 at update 1,600. Its HQ 0.904053 passes the precision bound; covariance still exceeds 0.85. Its parent endpoint covariance was 2.386427 with HQ 0.842285. Saved samples remain anisotropic: tangent variance is 2.08–3.88 times target variance while radial variance is 0.16–0.63 times target. All 256 mapped prior means lie within a target 3-sigma region. A local Jacobian approximation assigns substantially more spread to amplified prior noise than to mean offsets; this is a diagnosis, not proof of a causal repair. See the [compact density audit](density-diagnosis.json) and its [read-only reproduction source](density_diagnosis.py).

Halving LR makes all three word acquisition gates pass, with 14/24 passing observations each. First confirmed acquisition occurs at update 834 for both gamma=0.1 children and at 5,001 for beta2=0.99/gamma=0.3. Beta2=0.99 policies also meet the full word endpoint checks; beta2=0.9 still loses exact inverse reconstruction at the endpoint. Acquisition PASS must not be interpreted as sustained word qualification.

All three lower-rate policies fail sustained pole transport. Beta2=0.9/gamma=0.1 and beta2=0.99/gamma=0.3 fail endpoint movement, with mean_abs 0.254783 and 0.109029 against 0.3. Beta2=0.99/gamma=0.1 passes its endpoint movement/gradient bounds but only at the last observation: one terminal pass versus five required. Its endpoint is not a qualified pole repair. The first round also exposed Gaussian and word acquisition failures and strong-penalty pole failures; all remain visible above.

## Decision and evidence limits

Close this finite constant-policy and half-rate search. Preserve the original baseline and failed outcomes; the registered two-round limit is reached. Schedule removal is supported as a clock repair, and the smaller LR improves selected density/word metrics, but neither supplies one global Tier 1 solution. No Tier 2 work, seed variation, scientific retry, selection or public-default change follows.

The next useful question concerns local generator stretch and convergence under constant updates.
