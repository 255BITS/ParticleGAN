# Adopt: ring16 at 6,400 updates, K3P/KA2 without the LR horizon cap, longer wall-clock limits (2026-10-16)

> Update: after merging develop's test dispositions the ring16 swap is `discriminator_stability` revision 12 (revision 11
> plus the swap); the "revision 10" below is the unmerged draft the measurements ran under, kept at
> `reports/forge/adopt-20261016/measured-view-draft10.json`. Measurements and publication:
> [reports/forge/adopt-20261016/README.md](../adopt-20261016/README.md).

User decisions after dig2 round 2, implemented as defaults. No GPU training was run and the published
leaderboard was not regenerated; every family must be measured on this source before scores change.

## 1. Counted ring16 = `ring16_acquisition_6400`

- `configs/forge/tasks/ring16_acquisition_6400.json` (ported from #433): 6,400 updates, schedule horizon 6,400,
  384 checks at the same 16.67-update cadence, declared limit 1,200 s. Thresholds, scorer, sampling law and the
  5-check terminal sustained rule are byte-identical to `ring16_acquisition` (pinned by
  `tests/test_ring16_cap_revision.py` and `tests/test_forge_adopt_20261016.py`). R1/R2's sustained-window rule is
  unchanged, not weakened.
- `discriminator_stability` revision 10 requires it in Tier 1; revision 9 is archived at
  `configs/forge/view-history/discriminator_stability-v9.json`. It is the only ordinary view that requires ring16.
- `ring16_acquisition` and every receipt graded under it keep their identity.
- Not changed: the Atlas/E22 scoped views (`tier1_policy_coverage`, `policy_cloud_coverage`,
  `policy_cloud_coverage_as_served`). They use their own 400/1,600-update ring16 variants and belong to the
  Atlas/E22 fill.

## 2. K3P and KA2 without `network_lr_horizon_cap`

- New cards `recipe-prior-refactor-k3p-v2` and `recipe-prior-refactor-ka2-v2`: identical to v1 except
  `network_lr_horizon_cap: null` (and editorial text). v1 cards are unchanged.
- `configs/forge/selections/family-revisions-20261016.json` declares K3P -> k3p-v2 and KA2 -> ka2-v2
  (`trainer_families.load_declared_revisions` validates that each supersedes the family's pinned candidate and
  differs only in the declared field). `forge inventory` (one selected configuration per family) now runs the
  declared revision. The measured pins in `family-current-v1.json` stay as they are until the new rows are measured
  and published, because a pin must match a measured row.
- Effect beyond ring16: the G/D cosine anneal now spans each task's own horizon on every task (for example
  20,000 updates on word smoke) instead of min(horizon, 1,600).

## 3. Wall-clock limits end tests on their update budget

- `experiments/forge/wallclock.py` (ported from #434) reports a wall-clock hit as NOT MEASURED (wall-clock),
  never FAIL. Its contention-aware extension is now on by default, with a `DEFAULT_POLICY` of raised operational
  limits. Update budgets and declared `timeout_seconds` are unchanged, so task identities, Atlas variant pins and
  archived receipts stay valid.

| Task | Updates | Declared | Operational | Hard ceiling | Basis |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| five_word_joint_smoke | 20,001 | 900 s | 1,800 s | 5,400 s | solo 0.039-0.041 s/update; slowest measured 0.069 s/update (1,372 s) |
| five_word_joint_hold | 4,000 | 300 s | 600 s | 1,800 s | rerun hit 300 s at 3,667 updates (0.078 s/update, shared) |
| ring16_acquisition_6400 | 6,400 | 1,200 s | 1,200 s | 3,600 s | slowest measured 233 s |
| ring16_acquisition | 1,600 | 300 s | 600 s | 1,800 s | slowest completed 228 s; hit ran at 0.19 s/update |
| grid100 / rotated100 / staggered100 | 7,000 | 3,600 s | 7,200 s | 21,600 s | completed up to 2,133 s; hits at 0.53 s/update |

- At the operational limit the supervisor measures seconds per update and extends once by the slowdown against the
  solo reference, up to 3x. It does not extend when no observation was logged, or when the measured rate cannot
  finish the declared updates within that limit (for example the one-update-per-hour cell-quota defect).
- Applies to the queue worker (`experiments.forge drain`) and to the selected intake of `attempt_coverage`. The
  policy-cloud intake keeps its declared limits. Turn it off with `FORGE_WALLCLOCK_POLICY=declared` or
  `attempt_coverage --wallclock-policy declared`.
- Results record `wallclock{declared_limit, base_limit, effective_limit, slowdown_factor, decision}`. A queue
  timeout row carries `wallclock_outcome: NOT_MEASURED_WALLCLOCK`.
- `drain --no-publish` skips the post-completion `compile_memory` hook, which rewrites `reports/forge` boards
  and held the GPU lease idle for over 20 minutes per request in #433.
- Not ported: #434's rerun overlay `reports/forge/wallclock-reruns.json` and its regenerated leaderboard.
  The overlay changes three leaderboard rows, so it is left to #434. Without the file, the overlay code does
  nothing.
- Studies `configs/forge/studies/adopt-20261016-<group>-study.json` (groups k3p, ka2, bcap, bispf, r1r2,
  gan-v3) measure one family each through Tier 3 of revision 10. Each owns its campaign
  `adopt-20261016-<group>-v1` (120,000 s). The declared view total is 47,520 s; the operational total is 59,520 s.
  K3P and KA2 use their v1 recipe as control.
