## What the existing local loss can and cannot do

[Local-v2 source](../../../../particlegan/kinetic_transport.py) compares kernel means at detached real-batch anchors yₖ, with hₖ equal to the fourth-other-neighbor radius and width multipliers 1, 2 and 4. Let pₖ be real kernel occupancy and qₖ fake occupancy. Its terms are ((qₖ−pₖ)/pₖ)². For a fake point xᵢ, a term's gradient is proportional to **(qₖ−pₖ) k(xᵢ,yₖ) (yₖ−xᵢ)/(pₖ² hₖ²)**. Thus descent attracts toward an underfilled anchor and repels from an overfilled anchor. This sign-changing spatial signal can theoretically broaden one region while contracting another; it is not simply a Jacobian shrinkage penalty.

It nevertheless contains no radial/tangent coordinate, component covariance target, smallest-singular-value floor or separated within-row noise objective. Isotropic kernels acquire direction information only through the finite anchor cloud. Self anchors make pₖ≥1/n, sparse anchors change the neighbor bandwidth, far-away kernels can have small derivatives, and relative normalization changes force size. These are source-derived possible explanations, not measured causes of the failed run. Finite features can match occupancies without identifying the full component covariance or tails. [GMMN (ICML 2015)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v37/li15.pdf) supports differentiable kernel moment training; [Gretton et al. (JMLR 2012)](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume13/gretton12a/gretton12a.pdf) establishes distribution identification for characteristic population MMD. Neither result certifies this finite data-dependent relative residual or its nonlinear Adam dynamics.

No already-supported Recipe field explicitly bounds both extremes of the generator's output-space local spectrum. [Odena et al., §4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1802.08768) provides a more relevant research mechanism: upper and lower bounds on directional output/input quotients. Its implementation adds perturbation draws and another generator batch; it is absent from the current public Recipe and is not admissible here as an existing knob. For this 4→2 generator, any future spectral formulation must respect the unavoidable latent nullspace rather than demand four positive singular values. A bounded output-plane spectrum would still not guarantee component density or solve Gaussian/words.

A meaningful future composition would need measured signs/magnitudes of auxiliary versus adversarial force and actual rounded displacement in broad and narrow output directions, using retained original training panels and checkpointed histories. Those panels and the 1,584 optimizer checkpoint are unavailable in this audit, so a causal force claim remains unmeasured. Do not manufacture targets from evaluator centers, substitute center-only sampling, or rerun the failed revision to fill that gap. All original full density, minimum-spread, acquisition and sustained gates would remain mandatory for any separately justified global recipe.
