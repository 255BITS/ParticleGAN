## Why search these changes

The last ordinary matched BCap configuration passed all three smoke questions and the first five quality questions, then failed `mode_hold`: 5 of 8 modes, HQ 0.999755859375, effective modes 4.520427227020264, and cover 0.625 at 1,200 updates. None of its 24 observations passed the joint gate; its passing terminal suffix was zero. High fidelity among the occupied modes does not repair missing coverage. Conservative/aggressive prior and critic motion plus the known cap strength and a weaker cap are substantive next comparisons.

That is an observed coverage failure, not a proven optimizer mechanism. The original attempt is `bfd92d6f12174f8289903745f545e83c`, revision `18096544dede6a02e09e34be118d13e5554caa5aba4216980dbb442e91761312`, source digest `bb31b3f77bee0199fa51df84856dd04171fb58baca7c9328270f3e2a7c67d62d`. Its [compact frozen receipt projection](../technique-receipts/bfd92d6f12174f8289903745f545e83c.json) binds the original receipts but is not reusable qualification evidence. The declared inventory archive `runs/forge/technique-inventory-v1/receipts-and-source.tar.gz` (SHA256 `0574554f0b98b46308c9fc282ff5a993748a4e0a1fc7568fad30aab6d38581e8`) was unavailable in the inspected worktrees. Its manifest has no checkpoint or retained cloud entries. A per-mode gradient/covariance diagnosis would need the original operational `mode_hold/state.pt`, retained samples, and critic weights. The archived numeric signature alone cannot establish that cause.

The ring construction uses finite narrow ramps, not an exact discontinuous mixture selector. Its analytic ramp-probability bound is 0.00558520; the finite numeric shape witness is close to the maximum covariance tolerance. It establishes representability at the declared tolerance in this measured cohort, not universal sampling robustness. The public Gaussian prior remains active even when output noise is disabled; the 12 rows are not twelve output atoms. Ring hold/extension may reuse this static target representation, while their 200-check acquisition, 1,200 hold updates and 300 extension updates remain separate training questions.

BCap's fixed output-noise population objective and the clean evaluation law are distinct. If a noisy generator exactly reproduces a target Gaussian, its underlying clean width can be narrower; that analytic observation does not prove the actual optimizer's path or an impossibility of passing a transient gate. The noisy AE/residual capacity observations cannot be relabeled as zero-noise Modern/release measurements without their own bound law check.

Trajectory, vector and image capacity witnesses owned elsewhere must be joined only when host/prior/serving identities are compatible. Full required-task training pass, accepted calibration and independent robustness remain independent of every representation certificate.
