# Independent audit of pre-G anchor fitting

The frozen [saved-state gate](../../artifacts/continuous-learning/round6/sample-anchor-prestart-saved44/summary.json) passes 44/44 dense checks across the 1324–1335, 1380–1395, and 1530–1545 branches: eight modes and HQ 1.0 at every check. The original PR84 controls pass only 9/12, 14/16, and 1/16. This is a rejection filter from three borrowed states, not cold acquisition or a sustained fresh run.

The [read-only verifier](sample_anchor_prestart_audit.py) checks all archived source hashes, exact original support and update records against the PR84 stationary replay, and the actual rates at every update (D/G 0.00425, prior 0.0085). Original and modified branches have equal final training RNG/noise state and one D/G Adam moment advance per outer update. The modified recorder reports three native batch checks and one owner/RNG preservation check per update. All 44 corrections converged, selected the joint fit, and decreased the current-bank anchor objective. There were 88 joint Jacobians and 88 nonlinear trials. Conditional hosts and fresh acquisition were outside this gate.

Source inspection confirms `PreStartRecorder.correct` changes the start of `fit_output_targets` only: it copies the saved **G network and prior** parameters into the live model immediately before the inherited correction. The D update, D state, and both optimizer moment states remain from the native update. The same active-quadratic target, 20-iteration/12-half numerical budget, and actual objective comparison are inherited. A nonconverged fit still restores G/prior to the pre-G state. This is relative minimum-increment fitting, not a zero-parameter penalty.

The separately archived [one-state parameter capture](continuous-evidence/round6-anchor-parameter-motion/manifest.json) is internally consistent. I recomputed the 24-rank pre-G clean-output Jacobian and its SVD from captured update-2401 parameters. The native proposal has norm 0.02187957, of which 0.02082168 (95.165%) lies in that Jacobian's local kernel. Fitting from the native proposal leaves local kernel norm 0.02084070 (85.113% of the accepted displacement). Replaying the **same target** from pre-G converges to maximum row error 1.43e-6 and leaves kernel norm 1.11e-6 (0.00858% of displacement); its archived fixed evaluation is eight modes/HQ 1.0. This verifies the affine carry mechanism locally. It does not prove long-run bounded parameter motion for nonlinear G or distribution fidelity.

The [audit receipt](continuous-evidence/round6-sample-anchor-prestart-audit/audit.json) records input hashes and the recomputed values. No new host training was run.
