# Beyond centroid coverage: distribution fidelity and scale

The current [sample-group anchor rule](sample_group_anchor.py) solves a specific support-allocation problem. It infers groups from a real minibatch using a complete pairwise-distance matrix and the largest MST edge gap, assigns one distinct generated particle to each inferred centroid, and pulls every remaining generated particle toward its nearest centroid. Its fixed-bank loss is

```text
L(Y; C) = (1/K) min_injective_a Σ_k ||y[a(k)] - c[k]||²
        + (1/N) Σ_j min_k ||y[j] - c[k]||².
```

Consequently every zero-loss cloud puts all particles **at centroids**, with at least one at each, regardless of duplicate counts. This is useful for nonlocal acquisition and the exact free-output rest proof, but it is not a distribution discrepancy. In the frozen mode-hold host, `N=12`, the real law has eight equal Gaussian components with coordinate standard deviation `.07`, and the late generated law has equal particle weights and output noise `.029`. Even if all centers are correct, the emitted per-component variance from a coincident particle is only `.029²/.07² = .1716` of the target variance. With one or two particles at each of eight well-separated modes, the closest integer allocation has four singletons and four doubles; its component-mass total variation from uniform is `1/6`. These are representational facts, not a proof that the GAN lacks a stable good-quality state. The [fresh-bank free-output result](sample-anchor-free1200.md) retained HQ on 2,400 checks but continued to move with sampled centroids; it did not measure mass, covariance, or conditional fidelity.

The shared demo stamp fixes 12 particles, whereas `configs/toy100/constraints_simple_regularization.json` specifies 20,000 particles for a different shared recipe. The current correction is restricted to unconditional, unregularized-prior mode hold; the conditional trajectory host takes its original update. Extending the rule by grouping unconditional output coordinates across conditions would be wrong: it could exchange mass between different values of the condition while preserving a pooled marginal. [Chemseddine et al. (JMLR 2025)](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume26/24-0586/24-0586.pdf) prove that ordinary joint Wasserstein distance need not control the expected conditional distance, while couplings restricted to keep the condition fixed do. A conditional extension would have to construct real and generated laws **at the same observed condition** (or a justified local neighborhood for continuous conditions), and disallow cross-condition assignments. That invariant is a requirement, not an achievement of this candidate.

## One distribution-level replacement worth filtering

Replace the *centroid-pulling loss* with a **noise-aware, characteristic-kernel MMD² between the real conditional law and the emitted generator law**, while retaining the existing distinct-group assignment only as a coverage-feasibility mechanism. Missing-group acquisition can use the existing discrete donor/MM proposal to enter the feasible set; once covered, a function-space MMD step should be accepted only if the actual emitted-law MMD decreases while coverage remains feasible. This is one constrained objective, not an optimizer gain or a learning-rate schedule. It does not claim that all eight-mode high-quality clouds minimize the full distribution discrepancy.

For the frozen unconditional host, write `q_Y = N^{-1} Σ_j Normal(y_j, .029² I)` and `p` for the real law. With Gaussian kernel `k_h(x,x') = exp(-||x-x'||²/(2h²))`, the population `MMD²_k(p,q_Y)` is nonnegative and equals zero exactly when `p=q_Y`, because the Gaussian kernel is characteristic. The real-real term is constant in `Y`. The real-generated and generated-generated terms can be integrated **exactly over the known output noise**: in two dimensions, their Gaussian factors use variances `h²+.029²` and `h²+2(.029²)` respectively, with amplitude factors `h²/(h²+.029²)` and `h²/(h²+2(.029²))`. This removes generated-noise Monte Carlo variance and makes the objective describe what the host actually emits. A single bandwidth could be declared from the initial real bank's median nonzero inferred inter-group distance and frozen for the test; it would be a data-derived design choice, not a theorem or a tuned winner. For conditional hosts, use the same expression separately at each observed condition and average over conditions; do not allow marginal reallocation across them.

At the *population* equality `p=q_Y`, this MMD objective and its functional gradient vanish exactly, so it has the right equilibrium-rest property. A fresh empirical real minibatch generally produces a nonzero stochastic gradient even at population equality; deterministic samplewise rest is unavailable without a special paired estimator or a decision rule. More importantly, **this fixed 12-particle host cannot attain `p=q_Y`** with equal weights and the stated noise, so it can only settle at a constrained best approximation, where the projected gradient vanishes. An exact zero-loss or time-uniform stability guarantee would be false. The coverage constraint also means any theory for unconstrained MMD flow does not automatically apply.

Why MMD here? [Belhadji, Sharp and Marzouk (AISTATS 2026)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v300/belhadji26a.html) derive weighted-particle MMD quantization flows and a mean-shift interacting-particle fixed point; their position-and-*weight* setting addresses probability fidelity, but its variable weights are not available in this frozen equal-weight host. [Cao, Wei and Liu (2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.10592v1) derive sample-computable KDE difference fields for MMD and prove identifiability for the idealized characteristic-kernel population flow. Their result does not establish finite-batch or finite-neural-GAN stability. Earlier, a direct energy-distance gate rejected too many useful cold proposals and accepted a warm HQ-degrading move even while empirical energy improved [in this repository](energy-signal-dynamics.md); MMD with a coverage feasibility check must still be tested against the same conflict. Likewise, the fixed Sinkhorn-drift proxy collapsed a distinct, initially covered 12-point cloud [in this repository](nonlocal-signal-filter.md). [He et al. (2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.12366v2) give balanced transport a clear distribution-level meaning, but explicitly leave regularized *finite empirical* zero-drift identifiability for `n≥3` open. Neither prior failure rules out the proposed constrained MMD objective, and neither paper proves it will pass this host.

The existing joint G/prior nonlinear target fitter could realize a successful free-output MMD proposal, but the mathematical scope must remain narrow. [Dumont, Lacombe and Vialard (15 September 2026)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.17167) show that Gauss–Newton fitting of a Wasserstein-gradient drift is a natural-gradient step under an invertible pullback metric. Their global convergence result concerns an implicit proximal scheme on a closed convex function class, with approximating network classes becoming dense and fit errors vanishing; it does **not** cover this finite shared MLP, nonsmooth inferred groups, stochastic banks, explicit bounded fit, or adversarial critic.

The smallest faithful filter is output-space only, on the same saved warm/cold supports and cloned native data streams as `sample_anchor_free1200.py`. Predeclare one data-derived kernel width; first check the exact noise-aware MMD change and fixed-draw quality for the existing nonlocal donor target on one cold bank and for a covered warm cloud. If either conflicts, stop. If both survive, run the same 1,200 fresh-bank unconditional clock with coverage feasibility, actual MMD acceptance and held-out MMD/quality diagnostics; only then test a joint neural fit. A conditional extension must separately repeat the comparison at each observed condition. No target means or HQ oracle enters the policy. The warm gate remains all 1,200 quality checks, and the cold gate remains the final five; record mass TV and within-group covariance on held-out samples as *diagnostics* rather than relaxing those gates. This is a proposed filter, not an executed result.

## Scaling limit

The present Prim MST materializes `B×B` distances and runs Python `O(B²)` work; the dense assignment uses a `K×N` cost matrix and a Hungarian solve. Exact MMD adds `O(B²+BN+N²)` kernel pairs per condition. At the mode-hold `B=128,N=12` these are cheap. At a 20,000-particle recipe, the generated-generated MMD term alone has 400 million pairs. The current joint fitter explicitly builds a `(2N)×(P_G+N·z_dim)` double Jacobian and computes a thin SVD; using the mode-hold MLP's 19,298 generator parameters and `z_dim=4`, `N=20,000` would mean about **3.97 billion entries, or 31.8 GB for that Jacobian alone**. No claim about a practical 20,000-particle implementation follows from the 12-particle success.

At that scale, a separate engineering design would need kernel approximations or structured sums for MMD and matrix-free Jacobian-vector/transpose-vector products with a trust-region solver for the neural step. Such approximations alter acceptance and numerical guarantees; exact whole-map objective and conditional-invariance checks would still be required. The current result supports testing a proper law-level objective in a small faithful filter, not importing a large-particle convergence theorem.
