# Data-local emitted-MMD free-output filter

A single real-local Gaussian bandwidth improves the earlier broad-kernel result, but the declared cumulative free-output controller **fails strict stationary stability**. It is not ready for neural training. The [evidence archive](continuous-evidence/round7-local-mmd/manifest.json) contains the source-bound first-bank and 16-bank continuation receipts and the posthoc population check.

The fixed width is 0.0312862404: `torch.median` (the lower middle value for this even-sized bank) of the strictly positive nearest-neighbor distances among the exact first cold real128 D bank. The same bank and width are used in the initial cold and warm cloud comparison; later sequences keep the width and accumulate each arm's own native real banks into an empirical target embedding. The generated law is an equal-weight Gaussian mixture at the clean particles. Its kernel expectations integrate actual emitted Gaussian noise analytically: cold update 1 uses σ=0, subsequent cold updates use the original 240-update linear warmup, and the saved warm cloud uses σ=.029. The donor step picks the globally best strict decrease among all generated donors and all real points observed so far, at most 12 replacements. A bounded 20-iteration L-BFGS then takes only an actual objective-decreasing output step. It optimizes `J=Eqq k−2Epq k`; the omitted real-real term is constant within each update, so every reported `J` difference is exactly the full MMD² difference. Neither true mode centers nor the HQ grade enters an update.

The first common-bank check passed its declared *progress* gates: the cold cloud went from 0 modes/HQ 0 to 7 modes/HQ 1 while full MMD² fell .611154→.047399; the warm cloud retained 8 modes/HQ 1 while full MMD² fell .041106→.010801. Seven cold modes is acquisition progress, not complete cold acquisition.

In the 16-bank continuation, cold reaches eight modes by update 2 and passes its final five checks, although update 4 briefly returns to seven. The warm saved cloud passes 15 of 16 checks: the first fresh real bank sends it from eight to **seven** modes with HQ .999756, and later banks restore eight. Thus the declared every-update warm gate fails. After a separate 16-full-bank prefix, a paired omitted seventeenth D bank retains eight modes/HQ 1, as does its ordinary counterpart. A branch with two initially omitted banks regains eight modes on its fifth total update and passes its last five checks. A +.35 output-coordinate error imposed after 16 banks regains eight modes/HQ 1 on its first subsequent update. These are output-space diagnostics, not native neural or optimizer results; the warm branch starts a fresh empirical embedding at saved step1324 and does not recreate the historical data available to a learner trained from step1.

The first warm bank actually contains all eight true modes, but mode 2 has only 10/128=.078125 of its samples, below one equal particle's mass 1/12=.083333. The initial clean cloud has two particles near mode 2. Global donor moves 8 and 9 reallocate both; move 9 removes the last covered mode-2 particle while strictly lowering empirical `J`. This is a finite-bank mass fluctuation, not an absent real component. Bounded L-BFGS retains seven modes. The candidate cannot claim pathwise stability from a fresh bank even with a proper distribution discrepancy.

A [posthoc diagnostic](continuous-evidence/round7-local-mmd/population-check.json.gz) evaluates the frozen rejected proposal against the analytic *true* eight-Gaussian law; target centers enter only here. Full-proposal population `J` improves .021312→.006635 despite the lost mode, and a separate real128 bank also estimates a favorable whole-proposal change of −.016591. Hence a confidence guard on **whole-proposal MMD improvement** would still accept this quality failure. Donor move 9 alone has harmful population change +.001133; one independent bank mis-signs it as −.000404. Sixteen later banks (2,048 real points) estimate its paired change +.000645 with standard error .000266 and a posthoc normal 95% interval [.000124,.001166]. That interval is diagnostic, not a time-uniform acceptance certificate. A move-level, independent-data guard would need evidence before acting; this experiment does not validate or implement one.

All quality scores use pinned late `.029` evaluation noise. In the cold σ=0 case, the per-mode variance ratios logged by the reused `quality()` helper also add late `.029²`; they describe external grading spread, not the cold emitted law. The clean nearest-center mass TV is likewise a diagnostic label count, not exact mixture-law TV. Equality of full probability laws implies zero characteristic-kernel MMD and particle gradient in the earlier analytic check; a zero gradient at finite learned particles does not conversely imply equal laws.
