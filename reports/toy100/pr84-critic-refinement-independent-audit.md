# Independent audit: PR84 empirical critic refinement

This is a scratch diagnostic for the fixed-target `mode_hold` host, not a production or all-host result. It preserves the scheduled shared prefix through update 1,000. Each branch then inherits the same weights, Adam moments, EMA, data stream, and noise streams. The refinement branch uses constant applied rates for updates 1,001 onward: D and network G `0.00425`, prior `0.0085`. The archived [warm evidence](continuous-evidence/pr84-critic-refinement-independent-audit/warm/manifest.json) contains the raw JSON for every branch, declarations, exact source bytes, and SHA-256 checksums.

The source declaration, live source, and archived source hashes agree for all 14 declared files. The four branches share warm-state SHA-256 `6cc79b6e0d11eafae176b68e7d9d8c26c02c886866370134cd70c864fe882e21` and identical noise receipts. The identity branch matches the separately run scheduled cold control. The original PR84 branch exactly matches the earlier independent PR84 warm archive in final state, observations, noise, all 220 diagnostics, and all 200 comparable update records. Refinement and original have constant applied rate ranges at the values above; both optimizer moment counters finish at update 1,200. The refinement receipt verifies 400/400 game-block RNG replays and 200/200 bank and fit RNG checks. No elapsed-time rate decay or quality-based selection is in the active refinement update.

This extra optimization is expensive. In 200 updates, it made 10,674 L-BFGS discriminator gradient evaluations on 1,024 real/fake pairs each (10,930,176 pair evaluations), plus 200 exact first-bank gradient checks and the ordinary three D and three G phase evaluations per update. The median fit used 51.5 closures; 11 reached the 80-closure cap. It selected the lowest finite *training-bank* penalized D loss, with no held-out or quality oracle. The D curvature bound applies to the original Adam proposal, not the subsequent fitted D change; D Adam moments are retained from that proposal. Bank construction and stencil calculations add forward work beyond the gradient counts.

The preceding exact three-branch saved-state filter passed 44/44 local checks. The warm result alone did not establish a durable hold or cold acquisition.

The subsequent source-identical [dense fixed-target hold](continuous-evidence/pr84-critic-refinement-independent-audit/hold/manifest.json) now passes **all 1,200 checks at updates 1,201–2,400**, with eight modes throughout and minimum HQ `0.939453125` at update 1,365. Its final live grade is eight modes/HQ `1.0`. The original PR84 control passes 1,111/1,200 late checks, first failing at 1,325 and reaching HQ `0.716064453125` and seven modes, even though its final grade recovers to eight/HQ `0.998046875`. The scheduled identity passes 1,200/1,200, minimum HQ `0.9970703125`.

I separately verified the hold's declaration and archived source bytes against all 14 frozen warm hashes, the generated observer hash, and the byte-identical preceding warm summary. The hold and warm runs have the same inherited state and exactly matching first 200 diagnostics and update records after removing timing fields. The original PR84 hold also exactly matches its earlier independent archive in final state, observations, noise, all 420 archived diagnostic points, and all 1,400 comparable update records. All four hold branches have identical noise-policy receipts; the refinement branch has fixed applied D/G/prior rates at `0.00425/0.00425/0.0085` over 4,200 phase observations, one Adam moment update per player per outer update, 2,800 game-block RNG checks, and 1,400 each of bank and fit RNG checks. Its G curvature factor stays strictly positive (`0.04682`–`0.41455`), so this is not an explicit freeze.

The hold required 66,918 extra 1,024-pair D fit-gradient closures, or 68,524,032 pair evaluations, plus 1,400 exact first-bank checks; 21 updates reached the 80-closure cap. The run reported 805 seconds on the single-thread CPU. Seven extra real minibatches per update come from a clone of the toy host's training sampler; the center tensor is passed only to that sampler, not used to choose a target-directed update. This requires host-specific sampling access and adds a substantial data budget.

This is finite-horizon evidence of stability after an inherited scheduled prefix, not perpetual stability or acquisition from random initialization. The active warm/hold adapter is limited to zero-input-noise CPU `mode_hold`. The cold-support extension separately covers nonzero native input noise and trajectory conditioning, but a cold acquisition verdict is not included in these artifacts. The original host's input-noise anneal and output-noise warmup occur before the fixed-noise hold; they are distinct from the demonstrated constant post-prefix learning rates.
