# Common instance noise in the paired Rp game: bounded local assay

The fixed common Gaussian observation channel produced a real tangential pull toward an empty ring mode, but the eight-update alternating continuation lost quality and did not acquire that mode. This is a **failure of this declared fixed-width local candidate**, not a general result about instance noise. The runs are copied-critic continuations from archived PR84 states, not from-scratch qualification.

The proposed change was to evaluate the original relativistic paired logistic D and nonsaturating G losses on noisy real and fake coordinates, with the existing b_cap applied to the same observed D inputs. Independent real/fake Gaussian perturbations were expanded into antithetic plus/minus views; each Rp call averaged **2N index-matched fake/real pairs**, keeping the host's elementwise pairing. This differs from averaging D logits before the logistic loss, as in the earlier common score-stencil candidate. The common observation width was fixed **before fitting** to half the minimum separation of groups inferred by MST from the actual 1325 real128 bank: 1.1279860476026753. There was one width, no target-center control, and no width or seed sweep. Native output noise remained .029 and late input noise remained zero. The saved Adam rates were D .00425, G network .00425, and prior .0085 (β₁=0, β₂=.999); the continuation never changed them. The existing D3/G.25 own-curvature bounds remained.

**Pairing erratum.** The original report called this a crossed 2N × 2N Rp average, and the immutable continuation declaration says “antithetic 2N by 2N paired Rp.” Both descriptions are wrong. `GANLoss(mode='rp')` subtracts equal-shaped 256-element logit vectors elementwise, then averages 256 softplus values. On the archived cold case, actual D loss .6954945326 equals the matched-pair scalar bitwise; the unexecuted 256 × 256 crossed-pair alternative is .7011656165. Actual G loss .8776110411 likewise equals the matched scalar; its crossed alternative is .8826049566. The [read-only shape and scalar audit](continuous-evidence/common-instance-noise-round10/pairing-audit.json) reproduces both. Frozen source, results, and historical declaration are unchanged; this correction concerns their description, not an experimental rerun. This issue is separate from the earlier V1 baseline broadcasting bug.

The first exact saved-state test used update 1325 after the original D phase. Its original sharp D gradient and bounded/unbounded G full states replayed exactly, and all nine one-step common-channel proposals retained eight modes and HQ ≥ .99097. That copied D had been selected only by fixed-bank penalized D loss. The companion 1539 state in the original two-state script was **mis-specified as an acquisition case**: it occupied all eight nearest-center cells, while two particles assigned to mode 2 were off support. Its numerical result remains archived as a state-selection failure and is not evidence about nonlocal acquisition.

We therefore used the independent PR88 audit's exact PR84 cold1200 endpoint. Its clean nearest counts were `[2,1,2,2,1,2,0,2]`: mode 6 really was empty, and every existing particle lay within .107 of an occupied center. The copied D was fitted once on eight native-shaped real/fake128 banks (40 iterations, 80-closure cap; 44 closures), using the same fixed observation channel. Separate held-out penalized D loss fell .863776 → .670147. Nine independent one-step G/prior Adam counterfactuals each began from the saved moments and used one actual-next-shaped bank or one of eight reserved G banks; these G banks were never used to fit/select D. The modified-channel and original sharp G losses both declined in each counterfactual, but none put a particle in the empty mode. One had six graded modes; the other eight had seven. HQ ranged .63965–.99561.

The predeclared directional criterion did pass: 36/108 surplus-particle/bank entries gained more than target σ=.07 along the owner-to-missing chord and ended more than .07 beyond their owner center. Removing radial motion gives the more precise result: all 36 adjacent-mode 5/7 donor entries had positive tangential displacement toward mode 6, with per-bank ranges below. The nearest post-step particle was still 1.935 from the missing center. In the second bank, mode-7 particle 10 moved tangentially +.301 but radially inward −.180; its fixed-draw particle HQ fell 1→0. This was a useful direction but not a quality-preserving transfer of an atom between modes.

Because the directional criterion passed, we performed the declared at-most-eight-update continuation. The warm continuation completed update 1325's G phase from its archived post-D state, then took seven full alternating D/G updates. Its first G proposal exactly matched the one-step assay (HQ .996826, G factor .78968). Quality later dipped below .9 at 1328, recovered at 1329, then collapsed: mode counts over 1325–1332 were `8,8,8,8,8,5,4,4`, terminal HQ .26758. The cold continuation took eight full D/G updates from the cold1200 saved state. Mode counts over 1201–1208 were `4,4,1,3,1,2,2,1`, terminal HQ .15161. A nearest-center label temporarily reached missing mode 6 at 1206, but the fixed noisy grade had only two high-quality modes and HQ .00488. Both branches completed without nonfinite values. D and G Adam moments advanced once per applicable outer update, and the stored learning rates were not changed. For warm, D's own-curvature factor was .082–.215 over the seven new D steps; this fitted critic with old D moments had limited immediate adaptation. Neither trace supports local stability or actual acquisition.

The copied critic was installed while retaining the saved D Adam state; there were no further inner critic refits. The eight D-fit banks were drawn from each saved stream before the continuation, so the earliest continuation D banks overlap those used to fit D. This favors the candidate and limits claims about generalization. The cold snapshot is after the host's final evaluation, so its subsequent banks are a source-bound offline continuation, not an exact replay of a nonexistent update 1201 in the original PR84 run. The warm first G bank and fitted D hash were replayed against the archived one-step controls. Fixed4096 noisy scores are diagnostic only; ring means entered only after the updates to measure coverage and tangential motion.
