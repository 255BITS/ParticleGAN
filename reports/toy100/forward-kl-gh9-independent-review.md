# Finite-GH9 whole-map rule: independent prelaunch review

Frozen pure-output source `forward_kl_gh9_stress.py` SHA-256 `adc5d6f6ded8e6cf297ee90aa0d540723becc632a601b93b5b9a4393cd344485` was reviewed before its native 16-update screen. This review does **not** grant a quality or neural-training pass. The root's fixed finite-objective Jensen/EM proof in `reports/toy100/finite-likelihood-descent-principle.md` applies to an exact EM step on the same positive-weight GH9 target, in exact arithmetic. Its convex-hull and cumulative-decrease conclusions are limited to a fixed target and EM/proposals that descend that target; an inherited native GAN fallback in a later neural adapter would need its own whole-map GH9 check.

The implementation uses the cumulative real-bank GH5 target to propose up to 12 donor replacements from the **current** D bank, followed by up to 20 GH5 EM steps. It evaluates the completed proposal on the cumulative GH9 target. Strict GH9 decrease accepts it; otherwise the code runs one GH9 EM step from the **original** cloud, accepting only strict GH9 decrease, or restores the original cloud exactly. The GH5 proposal may have intermediate GH9 increases; those are measured and do not become applied states. Equality triggers the GH9 fallback, so a GH5 no-op cannot suppress an available GH9 EM signal. The frozen tests cover this branch, exact rest, and first-native-bank strict descent. Root's chunked donor evaluates the same GH5 objective with a bounded candidate matrix and verifies selected moves against the full GH5 cost. No source-level blocker was found.

This yields a per-update descent/rest property for the **declared finite GH9 objective while the target history is fixed at that update**. Arrival of the next data bank changes the objective, so it is not a cross-update monotonicity proof. GH9 does not certify the continuous blurred-target integral; GH41, if logged, is another numerical audit. The current-bank donor restriction also prevents a global support-acquisition theorem.

The source-bound synthetic check in `forward_kl_fixed_target_counterexample.py` records that last limitation at [`receipt.json`](continuous-evidence/forward-kl-fixed-target-counterexample/receipt.json). Its fixed finite target has 128 rows each at `x=−2,0,+2`; the current donor bank is the 128 center rows. All 12 model atoms start at `x=0`, with frozen `h=.031286240422040236`, `σ=.029`. On the exact frozen GH9 method, the GH5 donor search, GH5 EM, and GH9 fallback all perform no move; the selected output rests byte-exactly, with GH9 gradient norm `1.97e−15`. Its GH9 loss is `728.73637`, while a 4/4/4 atom allocation at the three target locations has loss `−2.83465`. This is a stationary symmetry trap with no nonlocal donor candidate in the current bank, despite a much better cloud. The construction is deterministic and highly special; it establishes **no probability or observed failure for the native ring sampler**. A bank containing a side-mode donor or an asymmetric perturbation can break the trap.

The root's chunked donor removes the prior `128 × full GH5 history` peak matrix, but full GH9 `cross_entropy` still materializes `81 × 128t × 12` float64 log kernels after `t` banks (about 1.19 GB at `t=1,200`, before concurrent arrays), and all-history work over a long hold remains quadratic in the number of updates. A neural long hold needs a separate cost/peak-memory check. No training run was launched by this review.
