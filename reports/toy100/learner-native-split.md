The fixed-support memory candidate's native `mode_hold` host produced the same complete state after two updates, whether run uninterrupted or stopped after update 1 and resumed with the complete host-plus-learner envelope. The two final envelope hashes are both `5c96a4e01ad33a25490be60b9441af7bdf4ed58d23fb7de3f74cd7df2cff9833`; the individual host and learner hashes also match. This is an exact resume/correctness check, not an acquisition or stability qualification.

The first bank was pending at update 1. A distinct second native D bank confirmed eight ordered groups at update 2, with counts `[31, 37, 34, 28, 30, 33, 31, 32]`. The independently initialized full and prefix runs had identical real pre-bank host and empty-learner hashes. The two per-update observations match exactly. The complete host hash covers models, optimizers, EMA, all RNG streams and the 1200-step noise policy; its step calls were 1 then 2. The complete learner hash covers pending and confirmed statistics, frozen identity centers, bank hashes and confirmation receipt. The applied constant rates were D/G `0.00425` and prior `0.0085`, with no annealing in the bound recipe. All three paths used the same generated host source SHA-256 `046c95d0333acdf0dd71b81d5691a1ce8fa4942e7a6760e6df9aba0b67ae20da`.
