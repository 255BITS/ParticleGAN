# Split-batch free-output time-stability diagnostic

A fixed 1,200-update output-space diagnostic tested whether held-out sampled `C+Q` acceptance alone prevents the delayed same-dataset quality failures. Each update drew one fresh native-law real128 batch from a copied saved data stream, used the first 64 rows for data-only greedy particle relocation and one fixed-assignment target, and accepted the exact target only when unit-mean `C+Q` strictly fell on both A64 and untouched B64. Otherwise the output cloud rested exactly. No GAN, neural fitting, optimizer, target-center lookup, schedule or adaptive retry occurred. Every step was graded with 4,096 fixed draws under the late `.029` output-noise law at diagnostic clock `240+t`. The warm support came from saved update 1324 pre-step; cold from saved update 1 pre-step. The real-only data stream does not reproduce native GAN draw chronology after the first batch.

| Saved support | Accepted / rested | Passing checks | First failure | Worst HQ | Final | Declared verdict |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Warm1324 | 407 / 793 | 1198/1200 | 59 | .884277 | 8 / .999023 | **FAIL** all-1200 hold |
| Cold1 | 342 / 858 | 1197/1200 | 341 | .877441 | 8 / .929932 | **PASS** free-output terminal five |

All checks in both runs retained eight modes. Cold first passed at update 1 and its final five checks passed, but three later individual checks failed. Warm failures were updates **59** and **873**; cold failures were **341**, **342**, and **1031**. All four new failures at 59, 873, 341 and 1031 followed *accepted* proposals that decreased `C+Q` on both halves. With the same evaluation noise held fixed across the old and new clouds, HQ changed `.942383→.884277`, `.991699→.899414`, `.912109→.884766`, and `.999512→.892578`, respectively. Their largest single-particle output motions were 6.268, 5.474, 5.817 and 5.487. Cold update 342 rejected its proposal and held the cloud from 341; its recorded HQ .877441 changed only with the evaluation draw. Thus split confirmation catches some overfit proposals but does not make sampled `C+Q` sufficient for strict same-dataset quality stability under this full-greedy free-output controller.

The [independent verifier](pr84_split_output_stability_audit.py) regraded all 2,400 fixed-noise checks, every split acceptance decision, the 1,200-real-batch draw schedule and final data-stream hash for each case, and reproduced both first-failure batches and their split objectives exactly ([warm audit](continuous-evidence/round6-split-output-stability/warm1324/audit.json), [cold audit](continuous-evidence/round6-split-output-stability/cold1/audit.json)). These are free-output diagnostic verdicts. They do not establish a neural method's acquisition, long hold, or impossibility of a different controller.
