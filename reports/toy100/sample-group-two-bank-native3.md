# Two-bank memory: three native neural updates

The [native diagnostic](sample_group_two_bank_native3.py) restored the exact passed update-2400 sample-anchor state and ran three mode-hold updates under constant nominal rates. The first two D real banks were ordinary native draws in every branch. Only update 2403's D real bank was conditioned: one branch omitted ring component 0; another retained exactly one component-0 point with a 0.2390-distance noise tail. The G real bank and original eight-component target stayed unchanged. These branches are conditioned diagnostics, not a target shift or an estimate of an unconditioned event probability.

All three branches had 8 modes and HQ 1.0 at updates 2401, 2402, and 2403. Update 2401 left G/prior at their pre-update point while the first D bank remained pending. Update 2402 confirmed 8 groups from two compatible native banks and converged in the joint G/prior target fit. At update 2403, both conditioned branches retained 8 fixed identities and converged in the joint fit. This checks a bounded response to one missing or isolated observation; it does **not** establish a long stationary run, cold acquisition, or recovery after a real distribution shift.

The [receipt archive](continuous-evidence/round7-two-bank-native-three-step/manifest.json) binds the exact input state, recipe, source snapshots, three final learner states, and all raw per-step records. The first two updates have identical bank hashes, native-game records, correction receipts, and grades across branches. Each third D bank replays bitwise across three gradient phases. Every branch advances each Adam optimizer exactly three times with nine recorded phase callbacks and ends with the same RNG state. The omission and singleton branches each changed only their third D bank.

The complete-bootstrap caveat from the [pure data filter](sample-group-two-bank-filter.md) remains: two compatible banks can both miss a true group and confirm an incomplete support set. Fixed references also estimate a truncated data law rather than the full target density. This three-update result cannot discharge either limitation.
