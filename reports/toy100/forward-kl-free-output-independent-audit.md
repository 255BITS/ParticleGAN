# Independent audit of the frozen forward-KL free-output filter

The frozen v2 [source snapshot](continuous-evidence/forward-kl-independent-v2/source/forward_kl_free_filter.py) has SHA-256 `0cc098d63efb2460dc038b897d5db532b9f87115ffbd5a78e42ee042ed21c873`. The exact [first-bank result](continuous-evidence/forward-kl-independent-v2/first-bank-result.json.gz) and [manifest](continuous-evidence/forward-kl-independent-v2/manifest.json) are archived. This is a **free-output diagnostic**: no GAN, neural weights, Adam state, host training update, or seed sweep was run.

The declared objective is the forward cross-entropy `−E_{X~p_h} log q_h(X)`, equivalent up to the fixed entropy of `p_h` to `KL(p_h || q_h)`. Here `p_h` is the 128-real empirical bank convolved with `N(0,h²I)`, and `q_h` is the equal-weight 12-atom emitted Gaussian mixture convolved with the same kernel, giving each component covariance `(actual_output_sigma²+h²)I`. The code uses `h=.031286240422040236`, derived once from the first cold native bank and reused for warm. The 2-D Gaussian log normalizer is `log(2πv)`, and the mixture contributes `log 12−logsumexp(−||x−y_j||²/(2v))`; both are correct. Gauss-Hermite locations `r_i+√2 h z_a` and weights `w_a w_b/π` integrate the stated convolved empirical target. [Independent tests](../../tests/test_forward_kl_independent_audit.py) match the exact analytic cross-entropy for a single Gaussian at orders 5 and 9, an exhaustive donor argmin on a separate small example, and an independently calculated fixed-equal-weight EM centroid and descent. **Five tests pass.**

Each accepted donor replaces one atom with one observed real coordinate and is chosen by exact global comparison for the fixed 5×5 quadrature. The implementation recomputes that same objective after replacement and raises if its cached log-sum-exp calculation disagrees. EM uses responsibilities for the same equal-weight, fixed-covariance model; normalizing the weighted responsibility for each atom yields the correct centroid M-step. The standard EM monotonicity argument applies to the finite positive-weight quadrature objective, and the implementation also checks actual nonincrease. A separate 9×9 rule audits accepted states and does not choose updates.

The first-bank checks pass their declared cheap gate:

Both use 12 donor replacements and 20 EM steps, with zero accepted-step 9×9 sign reversals. The cold 5×5 final gradient norm is `.002330`, whereas the 9×9 gradient norm is `.068972` (maximum coordinate gap `.038696`); warm norms are `.004875` and `.008855`. Thus the bounded 5×5 EM result is **not** demonstrated stationary for finer quadrature or continuous forward KL, despite agreement of accepted-step objective signs. In cold, the optimized emitted law uses σ=0; the offline HQ and reported within-mode variance ratios always use the fixed *late* `.029` output noise. For a singleton cold atom, its actual within-component emitted variance is zero, while the reported late-noise ratio is `(.029/.07)²=.171633`. These diagnostics must not be relabeled as cold-law variance.

The source clones each saved data RNG into an explicit generator, draws exactly the first native D real128 bank separately for cold and warm, and records both bank bytes and post-bank RNG hashes. Independent replay reproduced those hashes and left the global RNG unchanged. The fixed real mode means enter the native fixture sampler and offline quality grade; they do **not** enter width selection, donor comparison, EM, or cross-entropy. The warm case receives its own next native bank, not the cold bank; only the data-derived bandwidth is shared. This first-bank test does not claim that either saved state carries a historically accumulated real-data estimator.

An important finite-memory failure mechanism remains even though these two banks contain enough modes. If a bounded empirical history omits a genuine component, forward KL over that empirical `p_h` has no loss for under-density at the absent component. An atom left there spends 1/12 of generated mixture mass where the observed target has essentially no mass; moving it toward observed data can strictly lower the finite-bank cross-entropy and erase an already acquired mode. Accumulating all prior banks protects an identity **only after it has appeared** and makes storage/computation grow. A rare sampled outlier can conversely attract an atom because the narrow local `h` makes uncovered empirical spikes expensive. These are properties of the finite empirical objective, not errors in the likelihood formula or claims that the population forward KL prefers mode loss. The 5×5/9×9 gradient gap is a separate approximation limit. No neural promotion or perpetual-stability claim follows from the first-bank pass.
