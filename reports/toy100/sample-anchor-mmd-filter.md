# One-bank noise-aware MMD falsifier

The proposed **single-width, unconstrained MMD replacement fails the cheap filter**. On the exact saved cold step-1 first real128 bank, the existing nonlocal sample-anchor MM target improves fixed-draw quality but raises emitted-law MMD² by 37.5%; a rule requiring immediate MMD improvement would reject acquisition. On the exact saved covered warm bank, bounded free-output MMD descent rapidly destroys high-quality coverage while lowering its own objective. No 1,200-step MMD filter or neural training followed.

Each case uses its saved `pre_step` model and data RNG. Its single real128 bank and initial clean support exactly match the corresponding first update of the [fresh sample-anchor free-output test](sample-anchor-free1200.md). The kernel is `exp(-||x-y||²/(2h²))`; `h` is the median positive pairwise distance among the **inferred centroids of this bank**, then frozen for all proposals in that case. This is one declared width rule, not a width or seed sweep. The model law is the equal-weight Gaussian mixture at the twelve clean particles with the host's late output standard deviation `.029`. Real-model and model-model Gaussian-kernel expectations integrate this output noise analytically; the real-real term is the fixed empirical V-statistic. Neither the known target centers nor the quality gate enters the MMD objective, anchor proposal, or local solver.

The warm local solver is an unconstrained 12×2 L-BFGS-B diagnosis with at most 20 iterations and 100 objective calls; it **hit the iteration limit**, so its endpoint is not called an optimum. Its accepted iteration sequence lowered MMD² at every recorded point. At the final point, 11 of 12 clean supports are farther than the host's `.21` quality radius from every true center, compared with none initially; the median nearest-center distance rose from `.0652` to `.5230`. The nearest-center *label* mass TV remains `1/6` even after quality collapses, so such a label count is not a fidelity measure for off-support particles. The broad inter-group kernel can drive a very small finite-bank discrepancy while losing the host's local mode quality. This is evidence against this particular width and unconstrained objective, not a proof against characteristic MMD at other scales or under a separately declared coverage constraint.

The identity check is deliberately one-way. For identical empirical point sets with zero output noise, MMD² and its particle gradient are zero. For identical Gaussian-mixture laws with the same `.029` noise on both sides, the computed MMD² is exactly zero and maximum gradient magnitude is below `2×10⁻¹⁸`. This verifies **equality of full laws implies rest** in the analytic implementation. It does not establish that a zero gradient at twelve particle coordinates implies equality of laws. On the actual saved warm real bank, the initial MMD gradient norm is `.00625` even though the cloud passes the HQ gate; a fresh-bank stochastic field cannot be assumed to rest samplewise. [Cao, Wei and Liu (2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.10592v1) discuss identifiability for an idealized *global* characteristic-kernel field, which does not supply a finite-particle converse here.

The next action is to keep the guarded sample-anchor candidate's warm/hold/cold host gates separate, and **not** promote a plain MMD acceptance controller from this result. A later law-fidelity design would need an explicit data-derived coverage feasibility rule plus a resolution-sensitive distribution discrepancy, with a fresh free-output filter before neural training. No arbitrary HQ-tied radius or new gain is introduced here. [Belhadji, Sharp and Marzouk (AISTATS 2026)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v300/belhadji26a.html) study MMD quantization with variable particle weights, a degree of freedom this frozen equal-weight host lacks.
