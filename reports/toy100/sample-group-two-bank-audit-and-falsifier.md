# Fixed-support memory: independent audit and incomplete-bootstrap failure

The [independent read-only audit](continuous-evidence/round7-two-bank-independent-audit/manifest.json) verified all 44 saved-state checks against the exact PR84 captured states, support points, and native records. The fixed-support candidate passes all 12, 16, and 16 checks in the three saved windows (minimum HQ 0.99902, 0.99902, and 0.96899; eight modes throughout). The original branch passes 9/12, 14/16, and 1/16. The candidate's first bank is pending with G/prior at rest, its second bank confirms eight identities, and later updates use unchanged reference centers. At every step the observed rates are D 0.00425, G 0.00425, and prior 0.0085; final Adam moment steps equal the window ends. Source snapshots, RNG equality between arms, first-D-record parity, three native replay checks per update, and one owner/RNG check per correction all agree. These windows each start with a fresh learner; they are diagnostic repairs, not a continued learned-memory trajectory.

The audit also reopened all three [archived native three-update final states](continuous-evidence/round7-two-bank-native-three-step/manifest.json) and verified their exact state and RNG hashes. The initial two ordinary updates have identical native records, correction receipts, and grades across the ordinary, third-bank omission, and third-bank singleton-tail branches. Mode-0 accumulated count is 31 after confirmation, then 53 in the ordinary branch, 31 when absent, and 32 with a single noisy observation. All nine checks are eight modes/HQ 1.0, with exactly three Adam advances per player in each branch.

The most important limitation is demonstrated by a separate [source-bound native falsifier](continuous-evidence/round7-two-bank-false-confirm/manifest.json). Starting from the same passing update-2400 state, two consecutive D real banks were conditioned to omit component 0. They inferred and confirmed seven groups. The model then fell from eight to seven covered modes at update 2402. At update 2403 the D bank again sampled the **unchanged full eight-mode target**: it contained 22 component-0 samples. All 22, and no other samples, lay outside the frozen reference balls and were rejected by the learner. The model remained at seven modes. HQ was 1.0 at each check because HQ measures precision of the modes still represented; it does not certify coverage. D/G moments advanced once per update, each D bank replayed identically through all phases, and final training RNG matched the ordinary control.

This conditioned three-update path is rare under the ring's IID sampler but is a valid counterexample to any pathwise claim that two-bank confirmation guarantees complete acquisition. The fixed-support rule has no mechanism to learn a real component after an incomplete confirmation. A further candidate needs evidence-backed discovery from rejected real mass while protecting established support against isolated Gaussian tails. That extension is unimplemented here; the present candidate should not be described as a complete solution to continuous learning.
