# Independent audit of local and cumulative emitted-MMD filters

The frozen free-output formulas are correct within their declared empirical-bank scope. For the Gaussian kernel \(k_h(x,y)=\exp(-\|x-y\|^2/(2h^2))\), integrating isotropic generator noise \(\epsilon\sim N(0,\sigma^2I)\) gives the source's p–q factor \((h^2/(h^2+\sigma^2))^{d/2}\) and q–q factor \((h^2/(h^2+2\sigma^2))^{d/2}\). I checked the full emitted MMD² against explicit two-dimensional Gauss-Hermite integration, and checked every generated-donor/real-target replacement delta against full objective recomputation at both zero and positive noise. The cumulative filter's `J=qq−2pq` drops only the real–real term, which is constant in generated outputs **within the same accumulated data history**. Its all-pair delta agrees with changes in both `J` and full MMD². Four independent tests pass in [test_local_mmd_independent.py](../../tests/test_local_mmd_independent.py).

The local width `0.0312862404` is the median positive nearest-neighbor distance in the first native cold D bank (SHA-256 `a2178917c754f20123a7c950d6e3cbaa8325114c20c4b0bda68c52d9e7ceeb19`). It is frozen across both saved clouds and every cumulative update. Cold update 1 uses actual emitted output \(\sigma=0\); warm update 1324 uses `0.029`. The cumulative simulation consumes one native D real bank, two prior-index draws, then a G real bank per update. Candidate moves use only observed real coordinates, emitted-noise MMD, and a bounded 12-donor/20-iteration (100-call) L-BFGS objective decrease. True ring centers occur in the simulator's real-data draw and external quality logging, not in bandwidth, donor choice, J, or solver acceptance. The quality callback passed to SciPy records rows and returns no decision to the solver.

The cumulative predeclared strict screen **fails**: its first warm update 1324 moves a passing eight-mode cloud to seven modes/HQ `0.999756`; the other 15 of 16 warm checks pass, with eight modes restored at 1325. Cold first reaches eight modes at update 2 and passes its terminal five checks; a paired omitted bank after 16 updates retains eight, two initially omitted banks later recover, and a fixed `+0.35` output error recovers by five free-output steps. Those separate successes do not erase the warm failure. The first warm whole update moves one particle as far as `5.558` output units and uses all 12 allowed donor replacements; the loss of a mode is consistent with a large finite-bank reallocation despite strict same-history J descent. This is not a neural, Adam, or long same-target stability result.

Two diagnostic labels need care. `quality()` grades both cold and warm clouds using the fixed **late** `.029` output-noise draw at diagnostic clock 240; its coordinate variance ratios add `.029²` even when the cold update's emitted objective uses `σ=0`. Its `mode_mass_tv` is based on clean nearest-center particle counts, not an exact emitted-mixture mass integral. Neither label enters update selection. The real-real MMD term changes when new banks are appended, so monotonic within-update J checks do not assert cross-update decrease of one fixed objective. A finite empirical bank also differs from the full real law; the separate same-law rest identity does not claim that this sampled target yields exact rest.
