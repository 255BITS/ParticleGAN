# Fixed four-projection discriminator, noiseless F5/H1600 grid100

Two predeclared scratch arms used the exact public-default noiseless F5/H1600 config (SHA-256 `51f6bd91…`), seed 1234, 7,000 updates, all five terminal 20k checks, and an independent 100k holdout. Both replaced only D's input representation with the fixed unit rows `x`, `y`, `(x+y)/√2`, `(x−y)/√2` before the Fourier-5 MLP. Its width/depth/frequency schedule stayed fixed; the extra input channels increase first-layer parameters, giving D 38,913 parameters versus 36,097 in the ordinary F5 control. The affine arm trained its identity-initialized 2×2 weight and bias (6 parameters); the translation arm froze that weight after ordinary affine construction and trained only its bias (2 parameters), as in the earlier translation probe. No target center or label entered either model.

The predeclared manifest, complete runs, source archives, and independent regrade (`artifacts/toy100-accuracy/affine-noiseless/projected-d-f5-v1-fc9410a/`) are retained. Scratch source commit `fc9410a08bdf8475ebfcfe3b7dd56214fef6bfe3`, source SHA-256 `063095fd…`; both runs declare `shared_gate_eligible: false`. Three focused construction tests passed. The saved construction receipts show byte-identical initial prior, G, D, latent/penalty streams, and projection matrix between arms. Each arm's model construction preserved its caller's global RNG state. The identity G weight and projection matrix remained unchanged in the translation arm. V2 source archives, all terminal/final/holdout file hashes, and every scored event were independently verified and regraded from saved point clouds with no mismatch.

Both projected arms' **100k live holdouts satisfy the four separate fidelity limits** (mass TV, center RMS, absolute trace bias, and radial KS); their complete accuracy gates remain **FAIL** because the frozen gate fails and no five-check terminal streak exists. The projected affine holdout has TV `.04922` and radial KS `.03922`; projected translation has TV `.04147` and radial KS `.03459`. At the final 20k check, affine center `.2036σ` and TV `.06005` narrowly miss the fidelity limits, while translation center `.2248σ` misses. More decisively, both remain below the frozen `.97` precision minimum and outside its `.4–1.7` covariance eigenvalue interval. The 100k holdout confirms those misses, so they are not just 20k sampling noise.

The new features materially improve centering and remove the worst F5 affine collapse, but conditional spread remains uneven. Mean trace is modestly **underdispersed** (bias `−.073` affine, `−.089` translation), while individual modes can be too narrow and too elongated. In the affine holdout, mode 2 has the smallest conditional eigenvalue (`.327` relative to the truncated target) and mode 46 the largest (`2.155`); the lowest mode precision is mode 50 at `.858`. In translation, modes 41 and 66 set the corresponding eigen extremes (`.327`, `1.926`), and mode 32 has precision `.870`. The frozen metric reports eigenvalues relative to the untruncated target variance, explaining the different numbers in the table. These per-mode extremes remain well outside its thresholds. The experiment supports diagonal covariance sensitivity as a centering aid, but does not provide a passing shared recipe.
