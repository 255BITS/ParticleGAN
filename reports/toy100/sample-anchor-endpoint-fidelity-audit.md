# Mass and shape of the strongest prestart anchor

The prestart distinct-anchor joint fit **acquires and holds all eight ring modes**, but its emitted distribution is much narrower and less balanced than the target. This is a read-only audit of the [cold update-1200](continuous-evidence/round6-sample-anchor-qualified/sample-anchor-prestart-cold-v2/mode_hold-final-state.pt.gz) and [own-acquired update-2400](continuous-evidence/round6-anchor-own-and-missing/sample-anchor-prestart-own-hold/hold-final-state.pt.gz) host states. The exact calculation and input hashes are in the [receipt](continuous-evidence/anchor-endpoint-fidelity/receipt.json); [this script](sample_anchor_endpoint_fidelity.py) reconstructs clean `G(prior.z)` without changing RNG. It uses known ring centers **only after training** for diagnosis. Both state hashes match their source-bound run reports.

| Endpoint | Clean particles per nearest mode | Particle mass TV to uniform 8 | Fixed 4,096-draw mass TV | Within-mode emitted coordinate SD / target SD |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Cold 1200 | `[1,1,1,1,1,1,1,5]` | `0.291667` | `0.281006` | `0.029 / 0.070 = 0.4143` |
| Own hold 2400 | `[1,1,1,1,1,1,1,5]` | `0.291667` | `0.281006` | `0.029 / 0.070 = 0.4143` |

The surplus particles are not spread out: the five clean particles in mode 7 are within `5.4e-7` at 1200 and `1.3e-6` at 2400 of each other. Each group's emitted covariance is consequently almost exactly `0.029² I = 0.000841 I`, versus target `0.07² I = 0.0049 I`; its coordinate variance is **17.16%** of target. The clean group-mean errors from their ring centers range `0.0138–0.0361` at 1200 and `0.0112–0.0496` at 2400. These are component-assigned mixture moments, using clean nearest-center groups; Gaussian boundary crossings are not measured. The fixed 4,096-draw mass figures are empirical and share the evaluator's fixed index/draw protocol, so their equality across endpoints is not an independent sampling replication.

The own-acquired hold's **all 1,200 update-by-update checkpoints** retain eight modes and HQ `1.0`. Their one-noisy-draw-per-particle support records show the same `[1,1,1,1,1,1,1,5]` allocation at every checkpoint. Only the two endpoints above are reconstructed from clean saved host states. The observed allocation is consistent with the current objective: it assigns one distinct anchor to each sampled real group and permits surplus particles to sit at any covered group, without a mode-mass or covariance penalty.

Even an optimally allocated **12 equal-weight particles across eight equal modes** cannot make mass TV zero: each mode's possible weight is an integer multiple of `1/12`, so its distance from `1/8` is at least `1/24`; summing eight distances and halving gives the sharp floor `1/6 = 0.166667`. Four modes with one particle and four with two attain it. This run's `7/24 = 0.291667` exceeds that floor by `1/8 = 0.125`. Thus the mass error has both an unavoidable small-toy discretization part and an avoidable allocation part. The shape error is separate: even the four-two balanced allocation would still need sensible within-mode spread to approximate the `0.07` target law.

These limits do **not** directly transfer to the production 100-Gaussian task. Its configured `20,000` particles divide evenly into `100` modes, allowing exactly `200` equal-weight particles per mode; the eight-mode integer-mass floor vanishes. Its target standard deviation is `0.03`, and the configured late output noise is `0.029`, so output noise alone supplies about `(0.029/0.03)² = 93.44%` of target coordinate variance, unlike the ring's `17.16%`. Production uses a different generator profile and a much larger joint neural fitting problem. The toy result establishes coverage and finite same-target stability for this small host, not production mass, shape, runtime, or long-horizon stability.

It should report per-mode mass TV, center error, within-mode covariance and reverse precision on a separate 100,000-draw holdout, alongside full coverage and sustained fixed-rate checks. Keep those fidelity metrics separate from the current ring HQ leaderboard. No production accuracy result for this anchor is claimed here.
