## Interpretation and limits

The global witness and bounded legal progress provide evidence to consider a
separate production implementation. They do not demonstrate an improvement in
physical means, noisy emitted features or any original quality gate. Nonlinear
D/psi, latent jitter and output noise separate that claim from this clean
finite-table objective. The learned D/EMA/FIFO share training history, so the
conditional iid concentration expression is empirical negative evidence here,
not an independent prospective or cumulative test guarantee.

Even empirical objective weights differ from the witness's natural odd-real
mixture weights. A global score does not certify each local direction. The
new CPU chart is distinct from the historical CUDA chart. The original count
pvalues were freshly calculated and thresholded at 3K+3, but clean FAST table
counts are descriptive and do not supply emitted-iid action authority.

The scratch prototype proves exact copy preparation/application. Production
phase ordering, complete trainer reset/rebase participation, counters and final
serving lease integration remain to be implemented and reviewed separately.
