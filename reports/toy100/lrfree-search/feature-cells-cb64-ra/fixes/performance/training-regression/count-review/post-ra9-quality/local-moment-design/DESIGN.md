## Why a direct local mean/covariance repair is unsafe

1. **Shared training data.** The current D was trained using the D-real FIFO; FAST/EMA parameters also depend on those observations. An even-fit/odd-test split after training does not make the head, table or directions independent of the odd rows. Naive Hotelling, permutation or normal p-values would need an independent fixed-head reference experiment. The current source already states its conditional-iid/adaptive limitation; adding moments cannot silently strengthen it.
2. **Aliasing and conditioning.** A group can span several modes or disconnected parts of support, and a cell can cut one mode. Its real mean may lie in an unsupported gap. Even-only topology cannot prove unimodality, and odd occupancy alone does not resolve it. Matching feature moments also need not match output moments under a nonlinear learned head.
3. **Finite power.** With roughly100 real fit rows in a local component, a .2-sigma coordinate mean residual is only about two standard errors under an optimistic independent normal approximation, before direction selection or many groups. A single omnibus discrepancy avoids100 separate rejection thresholds for detecting one aggregate mismatch. It does not prove that each local residual is nonzero, assign reliable local action directions, or control a worst-region covariance gate. Data-mass weights also do not equal a benchmark's equal-component weights when support is rare.
4. **Clean versus emitted law.** The target for clean anchors is not the real covariance. Independent additive output noise contributes variance, and latent jitter through G contributes further mean/covariance. Their group-conditioned cross terms need not vanish. The frozen RA8 diagnostic measured substantial negative conditional clean/noise covariance. Subtracting sigma-squared from a conditionally assigned real covariance is not generally valid. Even an unconditioned subtraction can leave a small or nonpositive variance budget with large estimation error.
5. **Updates and state.** A transport must share the existing5% ordinary budget, protect rare supported mass, and update paired FAST/EMA geometry. Every changed row must reach the population certificate invalidation and own-evidence reset hook; the existing95% survival threshold remains. Rank-at-most8 and four existing Newton passes bound a proposal, but do not make its statistical target sound. Edited latent coordinates need an explicit momentum/history and lineage policy. New semantic law/state must be typed, versioned, atomic on rejection and exactly replayable; no copied ancestral evidence, ephemeral-chart load dependence, extra serving override or changed noise rule.
