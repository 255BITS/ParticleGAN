## Interpretation

**Sample sizes.** Every cell is one deterministic run (no seeds). (A) uses 10 mode_hold pairs (5 at constant LR: PR202 K3P at lr .00425/.002/.001/.0005 and RP15-const; 5 with controller-driven LR: DV12, DV12 rc2.0/2.5/3, RP15), 26 image cells and 34 vector cells after de-duplication (snapshot 2026-09-27; rerun the script as more runs complete). (B) has **one** matched pair: PR202 K3P at constant lr .00425. Its ring_shift seg1 and stationary runs are the same trajectory up to update 2400 (checked bitwise on steps/modes/HQ/pass), so B is really one shared 0-2400 stretch plus two continuations: 2400-4600 on the ring and 2400-7500 on stationary.

**(A) Short-horizon cycling (the constant-LR oscillation from the 96-config sweep): AMSGrad does not fix it.**
- mode_hold `cyc`: AMSGrad is lower in 3/10 pairs and higher in 7/10, median delta +0.024. Constant-LR pairs only: 2/5 lower, median +0.018. `down_fh` (HQ decreases only) is mixed: 3/5 constant-LR pairs lower, median -0.24. `drops2_fh`: 2 lower, 3 higher, 5 tied.
- Headline cell: pr202-const-ams mode_hold uses the config that is perfectly clean on stationary, yet its last five checks go 6m/1.00, 3m/0.33, 6m/1.00, 6m/1.00, 0m/0.00 (`cyc` .292 vs plain .139). Plain lr .00425 also collapses late (7m/0.84 to 0m/0.00 at 900). Both fail the task.
- Acquisition on mode_hold is worse with AMSGrad: 2/10 runs arrive, against 5/10 for plain, and 2/10 pass at the final check, against 4/10.
- Images and vectors show no effect. Image `cyc`: 12 lower, 11 higher, 3 tied (median delta 0.000). Departures after arrival total 10 for AMSGrad and 12 for plain on images, and 11 and 15 on vectors. No deep (HQ<.5) departures on either side.

**(B) Long-horizon departures after a clean hold: AMSGrad removes them in the one matched pair.**
- Stationary 7500: AMSGrad passes 696/696 checks after arrival, with 0 departures in 6950 updates and minimum HQ .92. Plain passes 574/701, with 9 departures, 5 of them deep. Four deep collapses reach 0-3 modes and last 480, 390, 90 and 170 updates. HQ std after arrival is .006 for AMSGrad and .275 for plain.
- ring_shift: in seg1, AMSGrad has 186/186 and plain 132/191, with 2 deep departures. In seg2, AMSGrad has 170/173, made up of 3 single-check blips at HQ .88-.90 with all 8 modes. Plain has 145/198 there, including a 480-update collapse to 0 modes.
- The timing fits the PR202 creep account. Plain's first deep collapse comes at update 1480, which is 980 updates after arrival and 900 after it recovered from a 60-update blip. On the ring's seg2, plain held clean for 1280 updates before collapsing at 3910. Collapses then keep coming after each recovery (stationary: 4370, 5790, 6480). This is a slow hold-then-break pattern, unlike the check-to-check swings in (A).
- Unmatched context (these runs differ in more than `amsgrad`): AMSGrad does not make deep departures impossible. pr202-ams-lr0015 collapses at 1950 (HQ .10, 6 modes, 120 updates), 1280 updates after arrival, which is the same signature. pr202-ams-rc3-lr001 collapses at 1780 (HQ .21, 4 modes). Over the four completed PR202-package AMSGrad stationary runs, both deep departures fall before update 2000, and there are none in the 22k updates from 2000 to 7500. The two plain stationary runs (lr .00425 and .002) have 4 deep departures between 2000 and 7500.

**Bottom line.** AMSGrad fixes (B): at the one matched setting it turns repeated late collapses into a clean 7000-update hold. It does not fix (A), the fast cycling on mode_hold at constant LR, and it slows mode_hold acquisition. These are two separate phenomena, and AMSGrad only addresses the slow one.
